## What this converter does

The data size converter changes digital storage and file sizes between units. It shows decimal units (KB, MB, GB, TB, PB, based on 1,000) and binary units (KiB, MiB, GiB, TiB, PiB, based on 1,024) side by side, so you can understand storage sizes, download limits and why drives look smaller than advertised.

## How to use it

1. Type the **size**.
2. Choose the unit you have and the unit you want.
3. Compare the decimal and binary values in the table.

## Decimal versus binary

- 1 KB = 1,000 bytes; 1 KiB = 1,024 bytes
- 1 MB = 1,000 KB; 1 MiB = 1,024 KiB
- 1 GB = 1,000 MB; 1 GiB = 1,024 MiB
- 1 TB = 1,000 GB; 1 TiB = 1,024 GiB

The gap grows with each step: 1 TB is about 9% smaller than 1 TiB.

## Everyday uses

- **Buying storage:** understand how much space a drive or phone will really show.
- **Cloud and email limits:** check whether a file fits under a 25 MB attachment limit.
- **Mobile data:** estimate how many hours of video a monthly data plan allows.
- **Internet speed:** convert Mbps (megabits per second) into MB per second by dividing by 8.

## Typical file sizes

- A photo from a phone: 2 to 5 MB
- A song (MP3): 3 to 10 MB
- An hour of HD video streaming: 1 to 3 GB
- A modern computer game: 50 to 150 GB
