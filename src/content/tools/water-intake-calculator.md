## What this calculator does

The water intake calculator estimates how much you should drink each day. It starts with a baseline based on your body weight, then adds extra for exercise, hot weather, and pregnancy or breastfeeding. The result is shown in liters or fluid ounces and as a number of glasses.

## How to use it

1. Choose **kg and liters** or **lb and ounces**.
2. Enter your **body weight**.
3. Enter your average **exercise per day** in minutes.
4. Choose your **climate**, and tick the box if you're pregnant or breastfeeding.
5. Read your daily target and the glasses that represent it.

## How the estimate works

The calculator uses about 33 ml per kilogram of body weight as a baseline for drinks, adds roughly 350 ml for every 30 minutes of exercise, 500 ml for hot or humid weather, and 700 ml during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Food provides roughly another fifth of the water your body needs.

## Easy ways to drink more water

- **Carry a reusable bottle** and refill it through the day.
- **Drink a glass with each meal** and one when you wake up.
- **Flavor it naturally** with lemon, cucumber, mint or berries.
- **Eat water-rich foods** such as fruit, vegetables and soups.
- **Set reminders** if you tend to forget during busy days.

## When you need more

- During and after exercise, especially long or intense sessions.
- In hot, humid or high-altitude environments.
- When you have a fever, vomiting or diarrhea.
- On long flights.

## When to be careful

People with heart, kidney or liver conditions may be advised to limit fluids. Follow your doctor's guidance if that applies to you.
