## What this tool does

This to-do list is a quick, distraction-free place to keep track of tasks. Add a task and press Enter, tick it off when it's done, and drag tasks into the order you want. Keep separate lists for work, home or shopping, filter to see only what's left, and print or copy a list when you need it on paper or in a message.

## How to use it

1. Type a task and press **Enter** (or the **+** button).
2. Tick the box when you finish a task.
3. Tap the text to **edit** it, or ✕ to **delete** it.
4. Drag the **handle** (the six dots) to reorder.
5. Use **To do** and **Done** to filter, and **Remove completed** to tidy up.
6. Use **New list** to make another list, and switch between lists with the menu.

## Tips for a to-do list that works

- **Keep tasks small and specific.** "Email Sam about the invoice" is easier to start than "Sort out finances".
- **Start each task with a verb:** call, write, buy, book, send.
- **Put the most important task at the top** and do it first.
- **Limit today's list** to what you can realistically finish; move the rest to another list.
- **Review it every morning** and every evening. Five minutes of planning saves an hour of confusion.

## The two-minute rule

If a task takes less than two minutes, do it now instead of writing it down. Use your list for everything that takes longer.

## Privacy

Your tasks never leave your device, which makes this list a good fit for personal reminders you'd rather not store in an online account.
