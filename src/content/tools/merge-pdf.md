## When to use it

Merge PDF is for any time several PDF files belong together: a cover letter and CV for a job application, monthly invoices for your accountant, scanned pages that came out as separate files, or chapters of a report written by different people. Sending one tidy document is easier for the person receiving it, and many upload forms accept only a single file.

## How to merge PDF files

1. Tap **Choose PDF files**, or drag your PDFs onto the box. You can select several at once.
2. Check the list. Each file shows its number of pages and its size, and the total page count appears above the list.
3. Put the files in order. The files are joined from top to bottom: use the arrow buttons, or drag them on a computer.
4. Need another document? Press **Add more**.
5. Press **Merge PDFs**, then **Download** to save the combined file.
6. Press **Start over** to merge a new set.

## A worked example

You are applying for a job and the form asks for one PDF. You have three files: "cover-letter.pdf" (1 page), "cv.pdf" (2 pages) and "certificate.pdf" (1 page, a scan). Add all three, move the cover letter to the top, the CV second and the certificate last, then press **Merge PDFs**. You get one 4-page PDF in that exact order. If the scanned certificate makes the file too big for the form, run the result through Compress PDF.

## Tips

- **Name files with numbers first**, such as "01 cover.pdf" and "02 report.pdf". They're easier to put in order, and you can check the sequence at a glance.
- **Check the page count** in the list before merging. A file showing far fewer pages than expected may be the wrong version.
- **Mixed page sizes are fine.** A4 and US Letter pages, or portrait and landscape pages, keep their own size in the merged file.
- **Photos or scans as JPG?** Turn them into a PDF with Image to PDF first, then merge.

## Common mistakes

- **Forgetting the order.** Files are merged in list order, not the order you opened them in. Look at the list once more before pressing the button.
- **Merging locked files.** Password-protected PDFs are marked in the list and can't be merged until they're unlocked.
- **Emailing a huge result.** Scanned documents add up quickly. If the merged file is over about 10 MB, compress it before sending.
- **Expecting edits.** Merging joins documents; it doesn't change the text on the pages.

## Your files stay with you

The files are combined inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, which matters for contracts, medical letters and anything else confidential.
