## What this tool does

Merge PDF combines several PDF files into a single document. It is perfect for putting a cover letter and CV together, joining scanned pages into one file, combining invoices for your accountant, or assembling chapters of a report. You decide the order, and the merged PDF keeps the original quality of every page.

## How to merge PDF files

1. Tap **Choose PDF files** or drag your PDFs onto the box. You can select several at once.
2. Check the list. Each file shows its page count and size.
3. Put the files in the right order with the arrow buttons, or drag them on a computer. Use **Add more** to include extra files.
4. Press **Merge PDFs**.
5. Press **Download** to save your new combined PDF.

## Tips for a tidy result

- **Name files in order** before you add them, for example "01 cover.pdf", "02 report.pdf". They will appear in the list in the order you select them.
- **Need only some pages?** Merge first, then use [Extract PDF Pages](/extract-pdf-pages) or [Delete PDF Pages](/delete-pdf-pages) to trim the result. Or split a document into parts first with [Split PDF](/split-pdf).
- **File too large to email?** Run the merged file through [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf).
- **Have photos or scans as images?** Turn them into a PDF with [Image to PDF](/image-to-pdf), then merge.

## Quality and formatting

Merging does not re-compress or convert anything. Each page is copied exactly as it is, including text, fonts, images and vector graphics, so the text stays sharp and searchable. Pages of different sizes, such as A4 and US Letter, keep their own size in the merged file.

## Why merge in the browser?

Most online PDF services upload your documents to their servers, process them there and keep copies for a while. toolwise does all the work locally in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library. That means your files stay on your device, merging starts instantly with no upload wait, and it even works on a slow connection.
