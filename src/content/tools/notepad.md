## What this tool does

The online notepad is a clean place to write anything: quick thoughts, meeting notes, drafts, lists or code snippets. It saves automatically as you type, keeps several notes, and shows word, character and line counts. You can open text files from your computer, save notes as .txt files, switch to a monospace font, change the text size, and use **Focus** mode for distraction-free writing.

## How to use it

1. Click in the page and start typing. "Saved on this device" appears after a moment.
2. Press **New note** to start another. Notes are listed by most recently changed, named after their first line.
3. Use **Save .txt** to download the current note, or **Open file** to bring in a text file.
4. **Copy** puts the whole note on your clipboard.
5. **Delete** removes the current note.

## Handy features

- **Monospace** lines up characters, useful for code, tables and ASCII art.
- **Text size** cycles between small, medium, large and extra large.
- **Focus** fills the screen with your note and nothing else.
- Statistics show **words, characters and lines** as you write.

## Good uses

- Drafting emails or posts before pasting them elsewhere.
- Jotting down phone numbers, addresses and quick reminders.
- Taking notes during a call or lesson.
- Cleaning up copied text: paste it here to strip formatting, then copy it back.
- Keeping a simple journal on your own device.

## A private place to write

Because notes stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere, the notepad is useful for personal writing. Remember that anyone using the same device and browser can open this page and see the notes.
