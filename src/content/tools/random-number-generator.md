## What this calculator does

The random number generator picks random numbers within any range you choose. Generate a single number or up to a thousand at once, allow or prevent repeats, choose whole numbers or decimals, and sort the result. It's quick, fair and runs entirely in your browser.

## How to use it

1. Enter the lowest number in **From** and the highest in **To**. Both are included in the range.
2. Enter **how many numbers** you want.
3. Choose **decimal places**, and whether to allow repeats and sort the result.
4. Press **Generate**. Press it again for new numbers, and **Copy** to copy them.

Presets fill in common choices: 1 to 10, 1 to 100, a dice roll, and 6 numbers from 1 to 49.

## Ideas for using random numbers

- **Games:** roll dice, pick a player to start, or generate a secret number to guess.
- **Classrooms:** choose a question number, a page to read, or random groups.
- **Giveaways:** draw a winning ticket number from the range of entries.
- **Decisions:** assign tasks or choose a random option from a numbered list.
- **Testing and research:** pick random samples, IDs or test values.

## How fairness works

Simple random functions can be slightly biased when the range doesn't divide evenly into the generator's output. toolwise uses rejection sampling: any random value that would cause bias is discarded and a new one is drawn. The result is that every number in your range has an exactly equal chance.

## Limits

You can generate up to 1,000 numbers at once. With **No repeats** on, you can't ask for more numbers than exist in the range; for example, there are only 10 whole numbers from 1 to 10.
