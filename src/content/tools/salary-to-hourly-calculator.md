## What this calculator does

The salary to hourly calculator converts pay between periods: hourly, daily, weekly, every two weeks, monthly and yearly. Enter what you earn in any period, set your usual hours, and see all the other figures at once. It works for comparing job offers, checking a contractor rate, understanding a pay slip, or working out what your time is worth.

## How to use it

1. Enter your **pay** and choose the period it's for, such as per year or per hour.
2. Enter **hours per week** and **days per week**.
3. Enter **paid weeks per year**: 52 if holidays are paid.
4. Read the full table of equivalent pay.

## Useful benchmarks (40 hours a week, 52 weeks)

- 15 an hour ≈ 31,200 a year
- 20 an hour ≈ 41,600 a year
- 25 an hour ≈ 52,000 a year
- 50,000 a year ≈ 24.04 an hour
- 75,000 a year ≈ 36.06 an hour
- 100,000 a year ≈ 48.08 an hour

## Comparing job offers

- **Hours matter.** A higher salary with 50-hour weeks can pay less per hour than a lower salary with 37.5-hour weeks.
- **Count benefits.** Pension contributions, health insurance, bonuses and paid leave can be worth thousands a year.
- **Commuting time** effectively lowers your hourly rate; some people add it to their weekly hours when comparing.

## Freelancers and contractors

Contractors usually need a much higher hourly rate than employees earning the same salary, because they pay for their own holidays, sick days, equipment, insurance and periods without work. A common rule of thumb is to charge at least 1.5 to 2 times the equivalent employee hourly rate.
