## When to use it

Use this calculator when shopping in a sale to see the real price you will pay and how much you save. It handles a single discount, a second discount on top (such as an extra 10% off sale items), and sales tax, so the final number matches the checkout.

## How to use it

1. Choose your **Currency**.
2. Enter the **Original price**.
3. Enter the **Discount** as a percentage, or tap a quick option such as 20%, 30% or 50%.
4. Optionally, enter an **Extra discount on top**, for example a voucher on sale prices.
5. Optionally, enter your **Sales tax** rate.
6. Read **You pay**, **You save**, **Total discount**, **Sale price before tax** and **Tax**.

## Worked example

A coat costs $80 and is 25% off. You pay $60 and save $20.

At the till there is an extra 10% off sale items, and sales tax is 8%. Enter 10 in the extra discount box and 8 in sales tax. The extra 10% is taken from $60, giving $54 before tax. Tax adds $4.32, so you pay $58.32 instead of $86.40, the full price with tax. You save $26, and the total discount is 32.5%.

## Using it with tax-free prices

If you shop somewhere prices already include tax, such as most of Europe, leave the sales tax box empty. The **You pay** figure is then the price at the till.

## Tips

- **Compare deals properly.** "Buy one, get one half price" is the same as 25% off two items. Work out the price per item to compare it with other offers.
- **Check the original price.** Some shops raise prices before a sale. If you can, check what the item cost a few weeks earlier.
- **Set a budget first.** Use the calculator to check the final price with tax before you reach the checkout.
- **Watch for shipping costs online.** A big discount can disappear once delivery is added.

## Common mistakes

- **Adding stacked discounts together.** Two discounts are applied one after another, so 50% off plus 20% off is 60% off, not 70%.
- **Forgetting tax.** In places where tax is added at the till, the shelf price is not the final price.
- **Treating savings as money earned.** Saving $20 on something you did not need still costs $60.

## Quick reference

Common prices after discount on $100: 10% off is $90, 20% off is $80, 25% off is $75, 40% off is $60, and 70% off is $30.
