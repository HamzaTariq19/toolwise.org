## What this calculator does

The discount calculator shows the price you will actually pay after a percentage discount, and how much money you save. It handles extra discounts on top of sale prices, such as "an extra 10% off everything already reduced", and can add sales tax so you know the final amount at the checkout.

## How to use it

1. Enter the **original price**.
2. Enter the **discount** or tap a quick percentage like 20% or 50%.
3. Optionally add an **extra discount** on top, and your **sales tax** rate.
4. See what you pay, what you save, the total discount and the tax.

## Examples

- A 120 jacket at **30% off** costs **84**; you save 36.
- An 80 item at **25% off plus an extra 10%** costs **54**; the total discount is 32.5%.
- A 200 TV at **15% off with 8% tax** costs **183.60** at the checkout.

## Shopping smarter with discounts

- **Compare the final price,** not the percentage. 40% off an inflated price can still be more expensive than a smaller discount elsewhere.
- **Check stacked offers.** Coupon codes, loyalty discounts and sale prices often combine, but each applies to the reduced price.
- **Factor in shipping and returns.** A cheaper online price may cost more after delivery fees.
- **Watch "up to" discounts.** "Up to 70% off" usually means only a few items are reduced that much.
- **Set a budget first.** A great discount on something you don't need is not a saving.
