## What this tool does

Reorder PDF Pages lets you rearrange the pages of a PDF into any order. It is handy after scanning pages in the wrong sequence, when a scanner reverses a stack, or when you want to move an appendix, a signature page or a summary to a different place in a document.

## How to reorder pages

1. Tap **Choose PDF file** and select your PDF.
2. Thumbnails of all pages appear in their current order.
3. Drag a page by its dotted handle to a new position, or use the arrow buttons to move it one place left or right.
4. Use **Reverse order** to flip the whole document, or **Reset** to go back to the original order.
5. Press **Save new order** and download the result.

## Drag and drop on any device

Dragging works with a mouse, a trackpad, a pen or your finger. On touch screens, grab the dotted handle rather than the page preview, so you can still scroll the page normally. If dragging is fiddly on a small screen, the arrow buttons do the same job with taps, and they are also fully usable with a keyboard.

## Common reordering jobs

- **Scanned back to front:** one tap on Reverse order fixes the whole document.
- **Two-sided scans in the wrong sequence:** move the back pages next to their fronts.
- **Moving a cover letter to the start** of an application pack.
- **Putting a signed signature page** in place after printing and scanning it separately.

## Related tools

If pages are missing from the document, add them first with [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf). To remove pages you no longer need, use [Delete PDF Pages](/delete-pdf-pages). Pages that are sideways can be turned with [Rotate PDF](/rotate-pdf).

## Planning a big reshuffle

For long documents, it can be quicker to work in two passes. First use Reverse order or move whole blocks of pages roughly into place, then fine-tune individual pages with the arrows. If most pages need to change, it may be simpler to split the document into sections with Split PDF and join them again in the new order with Merge PDF.

## Quality and privacy

Pages are copied without any re-compression, so the reordered PDF looks exactly like the original and text stays searchable. The whole process runs in your browser; your document is never uploaded.
