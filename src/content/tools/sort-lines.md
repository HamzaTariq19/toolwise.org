## What this tool does

Sort Lines puts any list in order. Alphabetize names, sort a shopping list, order prices from lowest to highest, list items from shortest to longest, reverse the order of lines, or shuffle a list randomly. Paste your list, choose a sort order and copy the result.

## How to sort lines

1. Paste your list into **Your text**, one item per line.
2. Choose how to **Sort**: A to Z, Z to A, by numbers, by length, reverse or random.
3. Choose whether to **ignore capital letters** and **remove empty lines**.
4. Copy or download the sorted list.

## The sort options

- **A to Z / Z to A:** alphabetical, using natural number order and your language's alphabet.
- **Numbers, smallest first / largest first:** sorts by the first number in each line, ignoring thousands separators. Lines without a number go to the end.
- **Shortest first / Longest first:** handy for finding long entries or building tidy layouts.
- **Reverse current order:** flips the list upside down without sorting.
- **Random shuffle:** mixes the list fairly, useful for presentation orders or random groups.

## Everyday uses

- **Bibliographies and reference lists** that must be in alphabetical order.
- **Glossaries and indexes** for documents and websites.
- **Price lists and results tables** pasted from spreadsheets.
- **Classroom lists:** alphabetize names, or shuffle them to choose a random speaking order.
- **Code:** sort imports, CSS properties or configuration keys.

## Capital letters

With **Ignore capital letters** on, "apple" and "Apple" sort together. With it off, capitals and lowercase are still sorted alphabetically, but when two lines differ only by case the capitalized one comes first.

## Tips

- Remove repeated entries first with [Remove Duplicate Lines](/remove-duplicate-lines).
- To sort columns of data, keep each row on one line; the whole line moves together.
- To count lines before and after, use the [Line Counter](/line-counter).

## Private

Sorting happens in your browser. Your lists are never uploaded.
