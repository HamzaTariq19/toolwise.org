## When to use it

Use this tool to turn ordinary text into tiny-looking letters you can copy and paste. It is popular for social media bios, usernames, captions, game names and decorative notes, and for writing simple maths like x² or H₂O where formatting is not available.

## How to use it

1. Type into **Your text**. The default shows "small text".
2. See your text in three styles:
   - **Superscript (tiny raised)**, like ˢᵐᵃˡˡ ᵗᵉˣᵗ.
   - **Small caps**, like ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴛᴇxᴛ.
   - **Subscript (tiny lowered)**, like ₛₘₐₗₗ ₜₑₓₜ.
3. Tap a style or press **Copy** to copy it.
4. Paste it wherever you need it.

## Worked example

You want a subtle tagline in your Instagram bio under your name. Type "photos and travel", copy the **Small caps** version, ᴘʜᴏᴛᴏꜱ ᴀɴᴅ ᴛʀᴀᴠᴇʟ, and paste it into your bio. It looks neat and different from the rest of the text.

For chemistry notes in a chat app that has no formatting, type just 2 and copy the **Subscript** version, ₂. Then write H₂O by typing H, pasting ₂ and typing O. The same trick gives x² from the superscript style. If you convert the whole of H2O, the letters shrink too, which is not what chemists write.

## Where small text comes from

Unicode, the standard that gives every character a number, includes small letters for phonetics, maths and old writing systems. This tool borrows them. Because they are real characters, they keep their look when copied anywhere, unlike formatting such as bold or font size, which is lost when you paste into a plain text box.

## Tips

- **Keep it short.** A few words in small text look stylish. Whole paragraphs are hard to read.
- **Use superscript numbers for footnotes and powers.** ¹, ² and ³ look neat in plain text, such as 10³ or a note marker.
- **Check on another device.** Some phones show missing characters as boxes, so preview before posting on a profile many people will see.
- **Mix with normal text.** Use small text for a label or tagline and normal text for the important part.
- **Try other styles.** For bold, script and other looks, use the [Fancy Text Generator](/fancy-text-generator).

## Common mistakes

- **Using it for important information.** Screen readers and search may not understand it.
- **Expecting every letter to shrink.** Gaps in Unicode mean some letters stay normal size, especially in subscript.
- **Using it in usernames you want people to find.** Others cannot easily type these characters to search for you.
