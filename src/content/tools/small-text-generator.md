## What this tool does

The small text generator turns ordinary letters into tiny characters you can copy and paste. Choose superscript (small raised letters), small caps (capital shapes at lowercase height) or subscript (small lowered letters). It is a fun way to add a subtle, stylish touch to bios, usernames, captions and messages.

## How to make small text

1. Type your text in the box.
2. See it in each tiny style below.
3. Tap a style to copy it. The button confirms with "Copied".
4. Paste it wherever you like.

## The three styles

- **Superscript:** ᵗⁱⁿʸ ʳᵃⁱˢᵉᵈ ˡᵉᵗᵗᵉʳˢ. The most complete style, covering almost all letters and digits.
- **Small caps:** ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘꜱ. Clean and readable, popular for usernames and headings.
- **Subscript:** only some letters exist in subscript form, so it works best for short words and numbers like H₂O.

## Ideas

- A tiny tagline under your name in a bio.
- Soft-spoken "whisper" text in chats.
- Footnote-style markers¹ or chemical formulas like CO₂.
- Discreet credits on posts.

## Tips

- **Keep it short.** Tiny text is harder to read, so use it for a few words.
- **Test your bio** on another phone before committing to it.
- **Usernames:** some platforms reject special characters in usernames even when they allow them in display names.
