## What this calculator does

The GPA calculator works out your grade point average from your letter grades and the credit hours of each course. It shows your semester GPA instantly as you add courses, and can combine it with your previous GPA to give your new cumulative GPA. It uses the standard US 4.0 scale, with an option for schools that award 4.3 for an A+.

## How to use it

1. For each course, choose your **grade** and enter the **credits** (also called credit hours or units). Adding a course name is optional but helps you keep track.
2. Use **Add course** for more rows, or the cross button to remove one.
3. Your **semester GPA** appears on the right.
4. To see your **cumulative GPA**, open "Include previous semesters" and enter your current GPA and completed credits.

## Grade point values (4.0 scale)

- A+ and A = 4.0, A− = 3.7
- B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7
- C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C− = 1.7
- D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, D− = 0.7
- F = 0.0

## A worked example

Suppose you took three courses: an A in a 3-credit course, a B+ in a 3-credit course and an A− in a 4-credit course.

- 4.0 × 3 = 12.0 points
- 3.3 × 3 = 9.9 points
- 3.7 × 4 = 14.8 points

Total: 36.7 points over 10 credits, so your GPA is **3.67**.

## Tips for raising your GPA

- **Focus on high-credit courses.** A 4-credit course moves your GPA more than a 1-credit course.
- **Know the retake policy.** Many schools replace a failed or low grade if you retake the course.
- **Plan ahead.** Use the cumulative section to see what grades you need this semester to reach a target.
- **Track individual assignments** with our [Grade Calculator](/grade-calculator) to see what you need on your final exam.

## Note on different systems

Grading scales vary between schools and countries, and some high schools add extra points for honors or AP classes. Check your school's official policy if your GPA is used for scholarships or admissions.
