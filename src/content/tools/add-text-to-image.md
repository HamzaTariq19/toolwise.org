## When to use it

Add Text to Image writes words onto a photo or graphic. Use it to add a caption or date to a photo, put a quote on a background for social media, label a product image with a price or "NEW", make a simple event announcement, or add a title to a slide or thumbnail without opening a design app.

## How to add text to an image

1. Tap **Choose image**, or drag a JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC file onto the box.
2. Type your words in **Text** (it starts as "Your text here").
3. Choose a **Font**: **Modern**, **Classic**, **Poster** or **Handwritten**.
4. Pick a **Color** and set the **Size**.
5. Choose a **Style**: **Plain**, **Outline** or **Box**, and an **Align** option.
6. **Drag on the picture to move the text** into place.
7. Choose **Save as** and press **Download image**.

## A worked example

You're announcing a bake sale on social media with a photo of cupcakes. Type "Bake Sale · Saturday 10am" on two lines, choose the **Poster** font, white, size 12%, and the **Outline** style so it stands out against the colourful icing. Drag it into the empty space at the top of the photo and center it. Download as JPG and post: the message is readable even as a small preview in a feed.

## Design tips

- **Keep it short.** A few words are read; paragraphs are skipped. The text box holds up to 300 characters.
- **Use contrast:** light text on dark areas, dark text on light areas.
- **Use Outline or Box** on busy backgrounds.
- **Leave margins** so text isn't cut off when apps crop images into squares or circles.
- **Two lines beat one long line** on phones; press Enter to break a title neatly.
- **Match the mood:** Handwritten for personal photos, Classic for invitations, Poster for announcements.

## Common mistakes

- **Text too small** to read on a phone screen.
- **Placing text over faces** or the main subject.
- **Low contrast,** like yellow text on a pale sky.
- **Saving as PNG** for photos, which makes large files; use JPG or WebP.

## Copyright reminder

Add text to your own photos or images you have permission to use. Avoid using other people's pictures, logos or characters.

## Private by design

Your image is edited in your browser and never uploaded.
