## What this tool does

Add Text to Image lets you write words on any picture: a caption on a holiday photo, a quote on a background image, a title on a thumbnail, a price on a product photo or a label on a diagram. Choose a font, size, color and style, place the text exactly where you want it, and download the finished image.

## How to add text to a photo

1. Tap **Choose image** and pick your photo.
2. Type your text in the **Text** box. Use Enter for a new line.
3. Pick a **Font** and **Color**, then adjust the **Size** slider.
4. Choose a **Style**: Plain, Outline or Box, and set the **alignment**.
5. Drag on the picture to position the text.
6. Press **Download image**.

## Design tips

- **Contrast first.** White text with an outline works on almost any photo. Dark text needs a light area or the Box style.
- **Keep it short.** A few words in a large size are more striking than a long sentence in small letters.
- **Use empty space.** Place text over sky, walls or blurred backgrounds rather than over faces or detailed areas.
- **One font is enough.** Pick the style that matches the mood: Poster for bold announcements, Classic for quotes, Handwritten for personal notes.
- **Check on a phone.** Most people will see your image on a small screen, so make sure the text is still readable when the picture is small.

## Saving your image

The text is drawn into the picture at full resolution. "Same as original" keeps the original format, or choose PNG for the crispest letters. Your original photo is not changed.
