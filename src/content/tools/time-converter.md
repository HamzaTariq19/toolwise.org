## What this converter does

The time converter changes a duration from one unit to another, from milliseconds to centuries. It's useful for timesheets, project planning, cooking, science homework, video editing and understanding very long or very short durations.

## How to use it

1. Type the **amount of time**. Decimals like 2.5 are fine.
2. Choose the unit you have and the unit you want.
3. Read the result and the table of all units.

## Common time conversions

- 1 minute = 60 seconds
- 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds
- 1 day = 24 hours = 1,440 minutes = 86,400 seconds
- 1 week = 7 days = 168 hours
- 1 year ≈ 52.18 weeks ≈ 8,766 hours

## Everyday uses

- **Timesheets and payroll:** convert minutes worked into decimal hours, for example 45 minutes = 0.75 hours.
- **Projects:** turn an estimate in hours into working days or weeks.
- **Video and audio:** convert durations between seconds and minutes.
- **Science:** work with milliseconds and microseconds in experiments.
- **Big numbers:** see how long a billion seconds is (about 31.7 years).

## Decimal hours versus hours and minutes

Timesheet systems often use decimal hours. Common values: 15 minutes = 0.25 h, 20 minutes ≈ 0.33 h, 30 minutes = 0.5 h, 45 minutes = 0.75 h.
