## When to use it

Flatten PDF makes the contents of a PDF permanent. Filled-in form answers, comments, stamps, signatures and other interactive layers are merged into the page so they can't be changed or lost, and they look the same in every PDF reader. Use it before submitting a completed form, sending a signed contract, archiving a document, or printing a file whose annotations don't show in some apps.

## How to flatten a PDF

1. Tap **Choose PDF file**, or drag a PDF onto the box.
2. Under **What should be flattened?**, choose:
   - **Form fields**: filled-in fields become normal page content. Text stays sharp and searchable.
   - **Everything**: fields, comments, stamps and signatures are merged into page images. Text can no longer be selected.
3. Press **Flatten PDF**, then **Download**. The file ends in "-flattened.pdf".

## A worked example

You've completed a 4-page rental application with typed answers and want to email it to a landlord. Some PDF apps on phones don't show form answers properly, and anyone could edit them. Choose **Form fields** and press **Flatten PDF**. The answers become part of the pages, look identical on every device, and can't be changed by accident. The text is still searchable, so the landlord can find your details quickly.

## When to choose "Everything"

- **Sticky notes and markups** from a review that must appear when printed.
- **Stamps and drawn signatures** added as annotations.
- **Documents for archiving** where nothing should be editable.
- **After cropping,** to remove the content hidden outside the new page edges.

## Why some apps don't show annotations

Comments, highlights and form answers live in separate layers on top of the page. Some phone viewers, email previews and older printers ignore those layers, so the recipient sees a blank form or no comments at all. Flattening moves everything onto the page itself, which every viewer shows.

## Tips

- **Flatten the final version only;** keep an editable copy for corrections.
- **Use "Form fields" when possible,** so text stays selectable and the file stays small.
- **Check the result** in your normal PDF reader before sending.
- **Compress afterwards** if "Everything" made the file large.

## Common mistakes

- **Flattening before checking answers,** then needing to retype the form.
- **Choosing "Everything" for a long text document,** which makes it larger and unsearchable.
- **Assuming flattening adds security.** For confidentiality, use Protect PDF.
- **Flattening a locked PDF.** Unlock it first.

## Private by design

Your PDF is processed in your browser and never uploaded.
