## What this tool does

Image to PDF turns your photos and pictures into a PDF document. It is the quickest way to send photographed receipts, homework pages, signed forms or ID documents as one tidy file, because almost every office, school and website accepts PDF. It supports JPG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos.

## How to convert images to PDF

1. Tap **Choose images** and select one or more pictures. On a phone you can pick straight from your photo gallery.
2. Arrange the images in the order you want the pages to appear. Use **Add more** to include extra pictures.
3. Choose a **page size**, **orientation** and **margin**. The defaults work well for most documents.
4. Press **Create PDF**, then **Download**.

## Choosing the right settings

- **Page size:** A4 or US Letter create a document that prints perfectly. Same as image keeps every page the shape of the photo with no white space.
- **Orientation:** Automatic turns each page to match its photo, so wide pictures get landscape pages and tall ones get portrait pages.
- **Margin:** a small margin looks neat when printed. Choose no margin for full-page scans.

## Tips for photographing documents

- Place the paper on a dark, contrasting surface so the edges are clear.
- Shoot from directly above, not at an angle, and avoid your own shadow.
- Use daylight or a bright room instead of the camera flash, which creates glare.
- If the final PDF is too large to email, run it through [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf). To combine it with other PDFs, use [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf).

## Image quality and file size

Each image is placed on its page at full sharpness. Very large photos are scaled to a maximum of 3,000 pixels on the longest side, which is still more than enough for a crisp print at A4 or Letter size while keeping the PDF a manageable size. Images with transparent areas are kept as PNG so the transparency is preserved.

## Your files stay private

The whole conversion happens on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, which matters when they show receipts, identity documents or other personal information.
