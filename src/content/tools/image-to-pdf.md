## When to use it

Image to PDF turns photos, scans and screenshots into a single PDF document. It's the quickest way to send a set of photographed receipts, a multi-page form you snapped with your phone, ID documents for an application, or homework pages a teacher wants as one file. A PDF keeps the pages in order, opens on every device and is accepted by almost every upload form.

## How to make a PDF from images

1. Tap **Choose images**, or drag JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC files onto the box.
2. Put the images in the order you want the pages, with the arrows or by dragging on a computer. Use **Add more** for extra pictures.
3. Choose the **Page size**:
   - **A4** or **US Letter** for documents you might print.
   - **Same as image** for pages that match each picture exactly.
4. Choose the **Orientation**. **Automatic** turns each page to match its image: wide photos get landscape pages, tall ones get portrait.
5. Pick a **Margin**: **No margin**, **Small** or **Large**.
6. Press **Create PDF**, then **Download**.

## A worked example

You photographed a 3-page rental form with your phone and the landlord wants it as one PDF. Add the three photos and check that page 1 is at the top. Set **Page size** to A4, leave **Orientation** on Automatic and choose a **Small** margin so nothing is cut off when printed. Press **Create PDF**: you get a 3-page A4 document, usually 1 to 3 MB, ready to email.

## Tips for clean, readable pages

- **Photograph documents flat and in good light,** from straight above, to avoid shadows and slanted text.
- **Crop first if needed.** Cropping out the table or floor around a page makes the text bigger in the PDF. Use Crop Image before adding the photos.
- **Use A4 or Letter for anything official.** "Same as image" gives odd page sizes that can print strangely.
- **Add a margin for printing,** because many printers can't print right to the edge of the paper.

## Common mistakes

- **Pages in the wrong order.** The list order is the page order; check it before pressing Create PDF.
- **Low-light photos.** Dark, blurry photos stay dark and blurry in the PDF. Retake them rather than relying on the conversion.
- **Expecting editable text.** The result is a picture of each page, not a Word-style document.
- **Emailing a 20 MB file.** Many mail services limit attachments to 20 or 25 MB. Compress large results first.

## Private by design

Your photos are turned into a PDF inside your browser and never uploaded. That makes the tool suitable for passports, bank statements and other sensitive documents.
