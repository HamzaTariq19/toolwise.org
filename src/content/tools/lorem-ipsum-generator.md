## What this tool does

The lorem ipsum generator creates placeholder text for designs, websites, apps, documents and presentations. Choose how many paragraphs, sentences, words or list items you need, decide whether to start with the classic "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…", and optionally wrap the result in HTML tags.

## How to generate placeholder text

1. Enter **How many** you need.
2. Choose **Paragraphs**, **Sentences**, **Words** or **List items**.
3. Tick **Start with "Lorem ipsum…"** for the traditional opening, and **Add HTML tags** if the text is going into a web page.
4. Press **Copy text**. Use **Generate new text** for a fresh variation.

## How much text do you need?

- **Website hero or banner:** one sentence of 8 to 12 words.
- **Card or teaser:** two or three sentences.
- **Blog post mockup:** four to six paragraphs.
- **Navigation and buttons:** use real words instead; placeholder text makes menus confusing.
- **Testing layouts:** generate a lot of text to see how pages behave when content is longer than expected.

## Good practice with placeholder text

- **Test extremes.** Try very short and very long content to make sure layouts don't break.
- **Use realistic lengths.** If real product descriptions are about 40 words, design with 40 words of lorem ipsum, not 200.
- **Never ship it.** Search for "lorem" before publishing; placeholder text left on live pages looks unprofessional and can hurt search rankings.
- **Consider real copy early.** Writing headlines and key text early produces better designs.
- **Accessibility:** screen readers will read lorem ipsum aloud as nonsense. Replace it before user testing with people who rely on assistive technology.
