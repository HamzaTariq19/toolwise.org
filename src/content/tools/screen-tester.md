## What this tool does

The screen tester fills your display with test patterns to check for problems. Look for **dead or stuck pixels** on solid colors, **color banding** on gradients, **backlight bleed and uneven brightness** on dark and gray screens, and **sharpness and scaling** problems on fine 1-pixel patterns. It also shows information about your display, such as its resolution, pixel ratio, color depth, color range, HDR support and refresh rate.

## How to use it

1. Choose a test. It opens in full screen.
2. **Tap or click** (or press → ) to go to the next screen and ← to go back.
3. Press **Esc** or tap **Exit** to finish.
4. Dim the room lights for backlight tests, and look from a normal viewing distance.

## What to look for

- **Dead pixel:** a black dot on every color, most visible on white.
- **Stuck pixel:** a red, green, blue or white dot, most visible on black.
- **Backlight bleed:** brighter patches near the edges on the very dark gray screen. A little is normal on LCD screens; big bright areas are a defect.
- **Clouding or uneven brightness:** blotchy areas on the gray screens.
- **Banding:** visible steps or stripes in the gradients, often caused by a 6-bit panel or color settings.
- **Blur or moiré** on the 1-pixel patterns: usually means the screen is scaled, or the resolution isn't set to the display's native resolution.

## Before you test

- **Clean the screen** so dust isn't mistaken for a pixel fault.
- **Set the native resolution** in your system display settings.
- **Let the screen warm up** for a few minutes.
- **Check the warranty:** many makers only replace screens with a minimum number of faulty pixels.
