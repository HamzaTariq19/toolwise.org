## What this tool does

The image compressor makes JPG, PNG and WebP files smaller so they upload faster, fit within email and form limits, and take up less space on your phone. It works by re-encoding the picture at a carefully chosen quality level and, if you want, reducing its dimensions. Photos straight from a modern phone are often 3 to 8 MB; after compression they are usually well under 1 MB with no difference you can see on screen.

## How to compress an image

1. Tap **Choose images** or drag your photos onto the box. You can add several at once.
2. Wait a moment while the images are compressed. A progress bar shows how far along it is.
3. Check the result: you see the original size, the new size and the percentage saved for each image.
4. Press **Download**. With more than one image you get a single ZIP file.

If you want a smaller or sharper result, change the settings. The images are compressed again automatically, so you never have to start over.

## Choosing the right settings

- **Quality** controls the trade-off between file size and detail. 70 to 80% is ideal for most photos. Go down to 50 to 60% for thumbnails or web previews.
- **Save as** lets you switch formats. WebP is usually the smallest and works in all modern browsers. JPG is the safest choice for documents, forms and older software. PNG is best for screenshots, logos and images with transparent backgrounds.
- **Maximum size** shrinks large photos to a set width or height. A 4000-pixel phone photo resized to 1920 pixels is still sharp on a laptop screen but is roughly a quarter of the size.

## Tips for the best results

- Compress the original photo only once. Compressing an already compressed JPG repeatedly slowly lowers its quality.
- For images with text, such as screenshots, keep the quality at 80% or higher so the letters stay crisp.
- If a result says "Already well compressed, original kept", the image was already efficient. Try a lower quality or a smaller maximum size to save more.
- When a website asks for an exact limit, such as "under 200KB", the [Compress Image to KB](/compress-image-to-kb) tool will hit the target for you.
