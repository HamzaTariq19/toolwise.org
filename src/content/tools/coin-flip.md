## What this tool does

This coin flip tool tosses a virtual coin and shows heads or tails with a spinning animation. It keeps count of your heads, tails and current streak, and shows your recent flips. Need lots of results? Flip up to 10,000 coins at once and see the totals.

## How to use it

- Press **Flip coin**, tap the coin, or press **Space** or **Enter**.
- The result appears under the coin, and your tally updates.
- Press **Reset** to clear your results.
- For many coins, enter a number under **Flip many at once** and press **Flip**.

## When to flip a coin

- **Making a quick decision** between two good options.
- **Choosing who goes first** in a game or sport.
- **Settling a friendly disagreement** fairly.
- **Teaching probability** in class: flip 10, 100 and 1,000 coins and watch the percentages get closer to 50%.

## A handy decision trick

If you're stuck between two choices, assign one to heads and one to tails and flip. Notice how you feel about the result. If you're relieved, go with it. If you're disappointed, you've just learned what you really wanted.

## Probability facts

- Each flip has a 1 in 2 chance of heads.
- Two heads in a row: 1 in 4. Ten in a row: 1 in 1,024.
- Over many flips, the share of heads tends toward 50% (the law of large numbers), but streaks still happen along the way.

## How the randomness works

Many simple online tools use a basic random function that is fine for games but predictable in theory. toolwise uses the Web Crypto API built into your browser, the same kind of randomness used for security keys, so results can't be predicted.
