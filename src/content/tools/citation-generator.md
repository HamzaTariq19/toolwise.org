## What this tool does

The citation generator formats references for your essays, reports and research papers in **APA 7**, **MLA 9** and **Chicago** style. Enter the details of a website, book or journal article and the citation is built as you type, with the correct order, punctuation and italics. It also suggests an in-text citation and lets you collect your references into an alphabetical bibliography.

## How to create a citation

1. Choose the **style** your assignment requires.
2. Choose the **source type**: website, book or journal article.
3. Enter the **authors**. Use **Add author** for more than one.
4. Fill in the title and the other fields. Only the fields needed for that source type are shown.
5. Press **Copy citation**, or **Add to bibliography** to build a full reference list.

## What details do I need?

- **Website:** author, page title, website name, publish date, URL and the date you accessed it.
- **Book:** author, title, edition (if not the first), publisher and year.
- **Journal article:** author, article title, journal name, volume, issue, page range, year and DOI if available.

Find these details at the top or bottom of a web page, on the copyright page of a book, or on the first page of a journal article.

## Examples

- **APA (website):** Smith, J. M., & Lee, R. (2023, April 5). *How sleep affects memory in teenagers*. Sleep Foundation. https://www.sleepfoundation.org/memory
- **MLA (book):** Smith, Jane Marie, and Robert Lee. *How Sleep Affects Memory in Teenagers*. 2nd ed., Penguin Books, 2023.

## Tips for good referencing

- **Add citations as you research,** not at the end, so you never lose track of a source.
- **Use reliable sources:** academic journals, books, government and university websites.
- **Check capitalization:** APA uses sentence case for titles, while MLA and Chicago use title case. The generator converts titles, but proper nouns like names and places should be capitalized in your input.
- **Keep track of length:** check your essay against the word limit with the [Essay Word Counter](/essay-word-counter).
