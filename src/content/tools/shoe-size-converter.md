## When to use it

Use this converter when buying shoes from another country or an international online shop. It converts between US, UK and EU sizes and foot length in centimeters, with separate charts for women, men and kids. It is also helpful when buying shoes as a gift and you only know someone's size in one system.

## How to use it

1. Under **Shoes for**, choose **Women**, **Men** or **Kids**.
2. Under **I know my size in**, choose **US**, **UK**, **EU** or **Foot length (cm)**.
3. Pick your size from the **Size** list.
4. Read **Your size in other systems**, which shows the US, UK and EU size and the foot length.
5. Check the full chart below. Your size is highlighted.

## Worked example

You wear a women's US 8.5 and want to order from a European shop. Choose **Women** and **US**, then pick US 8.5. The result shows UK 6, EU 39 and a foot length of 25.5 cm.

For a man who wears a US 10, choose **Men** and pick US 10. That is UK 9.5 and EU 44, for a foot about 28 cm long.

If you are not sure of your size at all, measure your foot, choose **Foot length (cm)**, and pick the length closest to yours, rounding up.

## Buying for children

Children's feet grow fast, often by a size every few months when they are small. Measure before every purchase and leave about 1 cm of space beyond the longest toe. Choose **Kids** to see toddler (C) and youth (Y) sizes alongside UK and EU sizes.

## Tips

- **Measure in the evening.** Feet swell during the day, so evening measurements match how shoes feel when you wear them.
- **Allow for socks.** Hiking and winter boots often need half a size more for thick socks.
- **Read reviews.** Buyers often say if a shoe runs small or large.
- **Check the return policy.** International returns can be slow and costly.

## Common mistakes

- **Using the women's chart for men's shoes.** US women's and men's sizes differ by about one and a half sizes.
- **Rounding down between sizes.** A shoe that is too short causes more problems than one that is slightly long.
- **Forgetting that EU sizes can vary.** Some brands use whole EU sizes only, so you may need to choose between two.

Foot length is the best guide when sizes do not match exactly. If a brand gives a chart in centimeters, compare it with your own measurement.
