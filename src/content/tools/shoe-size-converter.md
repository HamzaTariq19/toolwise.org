## Why sizes differ between countries

Shoe sizes grew from different measuring traditions. UK sizes are based on barleycorns (a third of an inch), US sizes follow a similar scale but start at a different point, and European sizes use Paris points (two-thirds of a centimeter). That's why conversions aren't perfectly neat, and why charts from different sources can disagree by half a size.

## Tips for buying shoes online

- **Measure your feet** and compare the centimeter length with the brand's chart, which is more reliable than converting sizes.
- **Check the width.** Many brands offer narrow, regular and wide fits.
- **Allow some space:** about a thumb's width (1 to 1.5 cm) between your longest toe and the end of the shoe.
- **Sports shoes** often fit smaller; many runners go up half a size.
- **Kids' feet grow fast:** check their size every few months.
