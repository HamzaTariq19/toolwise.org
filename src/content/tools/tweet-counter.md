## What this tool does

This character counter for X (formerly Twitter) shows exactly how many characters your post uses and how many you have left, using the same counting rules as X. When your text is too long, it automatically splits it into a numbered thread that you can copy post by post.

## How to use it

1. Type or paste your post.
2. Watch the ring and the **characters left**. It turns amber near the limit and red when you go over.
3. If you're over 280, scroll to **Split into a thread** and copy each part.

## How X counts characters

- Most letters, numbers, punctuation and spaces count as **1**.
- **Emoji** count as **2**, even complex ones like flags and family emoji.
- **Chinese, Japanese and Korean** characters count as **2**.
- Every **link** counts as **23**.

## Tips for better posts

- **Lead with the point.** The first line decides whether people keep reading.
- **One or two hashtags** at most; more can reduce engagement.
- **Use line breaks** to make longer posts easy to scan.
- **Threads:** make the first post work on its own and tease what follows.
- **Images and videos** don't use characters, so let them carry some of the message.
