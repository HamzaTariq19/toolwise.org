## What this calculator does

The calorie calculator estimates how many calories you need each day to maintain your current weight, based on your body and how active you are. It also shows calorie targets for gradual weight loss or weight gain, and your basal metabolic rate, the energy your body uses at rest.

## How to use it

1. Choose **metric** or **US** units.
2. Select your **sex** and enter your **age**, **height** and **weight**.
3. Choose your **activity level**.
4. Read your maintenance calories and the targets for your goal.

## Choosing your activity level

- **Little or no exercise:** desk job and little intentional exercise.
- **Light:** light exercise or sport 1 to 3 days a week.
- **Moderate:** moderate exercise 3 to 5 days a week.
- **Active:** hard exercise 6 to 7 days a week.
- **Very active:** physical work plus training, or training twice a day.

Most people overestimate their activity. If you're unsure, choose the lower option.

## Using your calorie target

- **Track for a few weeks.** Weigh yourself at the same time each day and look at the weekly average. If it doesn't move as expected, adjust by 100 to 200 calories.
- **Prioritize protein and fiber,** which help you feel full on fewer calories.
- **Combine with strength training** to keep muscle while losing fat.
- **Aim for slow, steady change.** Losing 0.25 to 1% of body weight a week is sustainable for most people.

## Who should not rely on this calculator

Pregnant or breastfeeding people, children and teenagers, athletes in heavy training and people with medical conditions affecting metabolism have different needs. Speak to a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.
