## What this tool does

Text to Speech reads any text aloud using the voices built into your device. Listen to articles while you cook, check how an email or speech sounds, proofread by ear, practice pronunciation in another language, or give your eyes a rest. The sentence being read is highlighted so you can follow along.

## How to use text to speech

1. Type or paste your text into the box.
2. Choose a **Voice**. Each voice shows its language.
3. Adjust **Speed**, **Pitch** and **Volume** if you like.
4. Press **Read aloud**. Use **Pause** and **Resume** or **Stop** at any time.

## Tips for better listening

- **Pick a voice in the same language as your text.** A French voice reading English sounds odd and mispronounces words.
- **Natural voices:** look for names containing "Natural", "Neural", "Enhanced" or "Premium"; they sound much more human.
- **Speed:** 1× is normal speech. Many people find 1.2× to 1.5× comfortable for listening to long articles once they get used to it.
- **Punctuation matters:** commas and full stops create pauses, so well-punctuated text sounds better.
- **Abbreviations:** voices may read "Dr." or "approx." in unexpected ways; spell them out if needed.

## Who finds it useful

- **Proofreaders and writers:** hearing text reveals missing words and awkward sentences that the eye skips.
- **Students:** listen to notes and essays while commuting or revising.
- **Language learners:** hear correct pronunciation and rhythm.
- **People with dyslexia or low vision,** or anyone tired of reading on screens.
- **Presenters:** time a speech and hear how it flows.

## Getting more voices

- **Windows:** Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices.
- **Mac:** System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage voices.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
- **Android:** Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output.

Reload this page after installing new voices.
