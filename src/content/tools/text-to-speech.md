## When to use it

Text to Speech reads text aloud in a natural voice. Use it to listen to an article while you cook or commute, proofread your own writing by ear, practise pronunciation in another language, help a child or a struggling reader follow along, rest your eyes, or check how a speech or script sounds before recording it.

## How to read text aloud

1. Type or paste into **Text to read aloud**.
2. Choose a **Voice**. The list shows the voices available on your device.
3. Adjust **Speed**, **Pitch** and **Volume**.
4. Press **Read aloud**. Use **Pause** to pause and continue, and **Stop** to end.

Voices come from your device, so the list differs between phones and computers.

## A worked example

You've written a two-minute wedding toast and want to hear how it flows. Paste it in, choose a voice in your language, and set **Speed** to 0.9× so it's close to a relaxed speaking pace. Listening, you notice one sentence is a mouthful and a joke lands too quickly. You shorten the sentence, add a pause before the punchline, and listen again.

## Tips for natural results

- **Use punctuation:** commas and full stops create natural pauses.
- **Write numbers and abbreviations the way they're spoken** when they matter, such as "twenty twenty-six" instead of "2026", or "for example" instead of "e.g.".
- **Slow down** to 0.8 to 0.9× for learning languages or proofreading.
- **Try several voices;** quality varies a lot between them.
- **Long texts:** break very long documents into sections for easier listening.

## Proofreading by ear

Hearing your writing read back catches mistakes your eyes skip over: missing words, repeated words, clumsy sentences and wrong homophones such as "their" and "there". It's one of the most effective quick checks for emails, essays and reports.

## Good everyday uses

- **Long emails and articles** you'd rather listen to.
- **Recipes** read step by step while your hands are busy.
- **Study notes** for revision on the go.

## Common mistakes

- **Choosing a voice for the wrong language,** which mispronounces everything.
- **Expecting a downloadable audio file.**
- **Pasting text with lots of symbols or URLs,** which voices read awkwardly.

## Private by design

toolwise doesn't upload your text; speech is produced by your browser and device.
