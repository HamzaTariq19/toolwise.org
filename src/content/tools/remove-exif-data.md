## What this tool does

Remove EXIF Data creates clean copies of your photos without the hidden information phones and cameras store inside them. Most importantly, it removes the GPS location that can reveal where you live, work or go to school. It also removes the camera model, date taken and other details you may prefer not to share.

## How to remove metadata from photos

1. Tap **Choose photos** and select one or many images.
2. Each photo is checked and cleaned. The result shows what was removed, for example GPS location, camera and date taken.
3. Press **Download clean photo**, or **Download all (ZIP)** for several photos.

Your original files are not changed; you download new copies ending in `-clean`.

## When to clean your photos

- **Selling items online:** photos of furniture or electronics taken at home can contain your home's exact coordinates.
- **Dating profiles and forums:** avoid revealing where you live or spend time.
- **Sharing photos of children:** keep their school and home locations private.
- **Sending files by email or cloud link,** which often preserve metadata.
- **Publishing on your website or blog.**

## How it works

JPG and PNG files store metadata in separate sections from the image itself. toolwise removes those sections directly, a lossless process that leaves every pixel untouched. Because phones store the photo's rotation in the same metadata, toolwise writes back only a tiny rotation note so the picture is not shown sideways. For formats where this is not possible, the image is redrawn and saved at high quality, which naturally leaves all metadata behind.

## Check before and after

Want to see what a photo contains first? Open it in the [Image Metadata Viewer](/image-metadata-viewer). Run the cleaned copy through it too to confirm the location is gone.

## Other ways to stay safe

- Turn off location tagging in your phone's camera settings if you rarely need it.
- Remember that what is visible in a photo, such as street signs or house numbers, can reveal a location too. Hide it with [Blur Image](/blur-image).

## Private

Your photos are cleaned on your device and never uploaded.
