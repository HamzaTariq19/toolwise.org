## What this calculator does

The debt snowball calculator builds a payoff plan for all your debts at once: credit cards, store cards, personal loans, car loans and more. It shows when you'll be debt-free, how much interest you'll pay, the order in which each debt gets cleared, and how much you save compared with paying only the minimums. You can switch between the snowball and avalanche methods to compare them.

## How to use it

1. List each debt with its **name**, **balance**, **interest rate (APR)** and **minimum monthly payment**.
2. Enter the **extra** amount you can pay each month on top of the minimums.
3. Choose **Snowball** or **Avalanche**.
4. See your debt-free time, total interest, interest saved and the payoff order.

## How the plan works

Each month, every debt receives its minimum payment. All remaining money goes to the target debt: the smallest balance for snowball, or the highest rate for avalanche. When a debt is paid off, its minimum payment is added to the money attacking the next one, which is why the payments "snowball" over time.

## Tips for sticking to the plan

- **Find extra money** by reviewing subscriptions and regular spending. Even 50 a month makes a difference.
- **Automate payments** so you never miss a minimum.
- **Celebrate each payoff,** but keep rolling the payment forward instead of spending it.
- **Avoid new debt** while you work through the plan.
- **Build a small emergency fund** first, so an unexpected bill doesn't push you back onto a card.

## Things to check

- If a debt has a 0% promotional rate that ends soon, plan to clear it before the rate rises.
- Some loans charge early repayment fees; check before overpaying.
- If interest is larger than the payments you can make, the calculator warns you; talk to a free debt advice service in your country.
