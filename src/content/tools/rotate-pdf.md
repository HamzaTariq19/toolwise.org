## What this tool does

Rotate PDF fixes pages that appear sideways or upside down. It is common with scanned documents, photographed receipts turned into PDFs, and files that mix portrait and landscape pages. Instead of tilting your head or rotating the view every time you open the file, you can save the correct orientation permanently.

## How to rotate PDF pages

1. Tap **Choose PDF file** or drop your PDF onto the box.
2. Small previews of every page appear.
3. Use the arrows under a page to turn it 90 degrees left or right. The preview turns with it, and the page number shows the new angle.
4. To turn every page at once, use **Rotate all left** or **Rotate all right**. **Reset** undoes all changes.
5. Press the **Rotate** button, which shows how many pages will change, then **Download**.

## Tips

- **Mixed documents:** a report with a few wide tables can have just those pages turned to landscape while the text pages stay portrait.
- **Upside-down scans:** press the same arrow twice to turn a page 180 degrees.
- **Double-sided scanning** sometimes flips every second page. Rotate those pages individually, or extract the even pages with [Extract PDF Pages](/extract-pdf-pages) if you need them separately.
- After rotating, you can tidy the document further with [Reorder PDF Pages](/reorder-pdf-pages) or [Delete PDF Pages](/delete-pdf-pages).

## How rotation works inside a PDF

Each PDF page has a rotation setting, measured in multiples of 90 degrees, that tells viewers how to display it. This tool updates that setting rather than redrawing the page. That is why the change is instant, the file size barely changes, and selectable text stays selectable. If a page was already rotated by its creator, your change is added on top of the existing angle.

## When rotation is not enough

If a page looks slightly tilted rather than turned by a quarter, the problem is a crooked scan, which needs straightening in a scanning app. If the page has extra white borders after rotating, trim them with [Crop PDF](/crop-pdf).

## Rotating on a phone

On a phone, the previews are arranged three to a row so you can see many pages at once. Tap the arrows under a page to turn it; each tap is a quarter turn. When you are finished, the download appears in your browser's downloads or Files app, ready to share by email or messaging app without any extra conversion.

## Private and secure

Your PDF is processed entirely in your browser. It is never uploaded to a server, so you can safely rotate contracts, tax forms and medical letters.
