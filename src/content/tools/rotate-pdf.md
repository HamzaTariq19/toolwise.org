## When to use it

Rotate PDF turns pages that are sideways or upside down so they read correctly. It's common with scanned documents, where a page went through the scanner the wrong way, photos taken with a phone that saved them sideways, and documents that mix portrait pages with wide landscape tables or drawings. Fixing the rotation makes a PDF far easier to read on screen, especially on phones, and stops recipients having to turn their heads.

## How to rotate PDF pages

1. Tap **Choose PDF file**, or drag a PDF onto the box.
2. Wait for the page thumbnails to appear.
3. Rotate single pages with the arrows under each page, or turn every page at once with **Rotate all left** or **Rotate all right**. Each click turns pages by a quarter turn (90°).
4. Made a mistake? **Reset** puts every page back as it was.
5. Press **Rotate PDF**, then **Download**. The new file ends in "-rotated.pdf".

## A worked example

You scanned a 12-page contract and pages 4 and 9 came out upside down, while page 7 (a wide table) is sideways. Open the PDF, press the right arrow twice under pages 4 and 9 to turn them 180°, and once under page 7 so the table reads left to right. The thumbnails show the result immediately. Press **Rotate PDF** and download "contract-rotated.pdf"; every page now reads correctly.

## Tips

- **Check the thumbnails, not the page numbers** printed on the pages; it's the thumbnails that show what will be saved.
- **Landscape pages can stay landscape.** Wide tables and charts are usually best left sideways in the document but turned so the text reads left to right.
- **Fix rotation before merging** documents, so you only check each part once.
- **Combine with other tools:** after rotating, remove blank scan pages with Delete PDF Pages or put pages in order with Reorder PDF Pages.

## Common mistakes

- **Rotating the wrong way.** Each arrow turns by 90°; a page that's upside down needs two clicks.
- **Expecting the view in your PDF reader to be saved.** Rotating in a viewer usually only changes your screen, not the file; this tool changes the file.
- **Forgetting landscape pages when using "Rotate all".** If only some pages are wrong, rotate them individually.
- **Uploading a locked PDF.** Unlock it first.

## Private by design

Your PDF is rotated in your browser and never uploaded.
