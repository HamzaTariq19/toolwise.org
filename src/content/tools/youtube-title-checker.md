## What this tool does

The YouTube title checker counts your title's characters, shows how it will look in search results, the home feed on a phone and the suggested videos sidebar, and gives practical tips: whether it will be cut off, whether you're using too many capitals or emoji, and where your main keyword appears.

## How to use it

1. Type or paste your **video title**.
2. Optionally enter your **main keyword**.
3. Watch the character count and the colored bar: green is ideal, amber may be cut off, red is over YouTube's limit.
4. Check the previews and the checklist, then refine your title.

## What makes a good YouTube title

- **Clear promise:** tell viewers what they will learn, see or feel.
- **Front-load keywords:** "Sourdough for Beginners: Easy Step-by-Step" beats "My Easy Step-by-Step Guide to Sourdough for Beginners".
- **Curiosity without clickbait:** create interest, then deliver on it in the video.
- **Numbers and specifics:** "7 mistakes" or "in 10 minutes" set clear expectations.
- **Match the thumbnail:** the title and thumbnail should work together, not repeat each other word for word.

## Testing titles

Write five or ten versions and compare them here. Read them on the phone preview, where most viewers will see them. YouTube Studio also lets creators test several thumbnails and titles on some channels.
