## When to use it

Speech to Text turns your voice into written text. Use it to draft emails and messages hands-free, capture ideas faster than typing, write if typing is difficult or painful, take quick notes while cooking or walking, or get a first draft of an essay or report down by talking it through.

## How to dictate text

1. Choose the **Language you will speak**, such as English (UK) or Español.
2. Press **Start dictation** and allow microphone access when asked.
3. Speak clearly. Your words appear in **Your text** as you talk.
4. Press the button again to stop. Edit the text by typing if needed.
5. Press **Copy text** or **Download .txt**, or **Clear** to start again.

## A worked example

After a meeting, you want to send a quick summary while walking to the station. Open the page on your phone in Chrome, choose English (UK), press **Start dictation** and say: "Hi team comma thanks for today full stop the launch moves to the fifteenth full stop". Fix any small errors by typing, press **Copy text**, and paste it into your email.

## Tips for accurate dictation

- **Speak at a steady, natural pace,** in full sentences.
- **Use a headset or get close to the microphone** in noisy places.
- **Say punctuation** if your browser doesn't add it automatically.
- **Choose the right language variant,** such as English (India) or English (US), for better accuracy with accents and spellings.
- **Dictate first, edit after;** stopping to fix every word slows you down.

## Helpful touches

- **Capital letters are added** at the start of your text and after each full stop, question mark or exclamation mark.
- **Spaces are added** between dictated phrases automatically.
- **Your language choice is remembered** on this device for next time.

## Common mistakes

- **Using Firefox,** which doesn't support speech recognition.
- **Blocking the microphone** by accident. Allow it through the icon in the address bar.
- **Dictating names and technical terms** without checking them; they're often misheard.
- **Background noise and music,** which reduce accuracy.

## Privacy

Speech recognition is provided by your browser, not toolwise. Chrome and Edge send audio to Google or Microsoft to recognise it; Safari may do it on your device. toolwise itself never receives your voice or your text.
