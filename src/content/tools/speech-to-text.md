## What this tool does

Speech to Text turns your voice into written words. Press the microphone button, speak naturally, and your words appear in the box as you talk. It is a fast way to write emails, notes, essays, messages and to-do lists, and a big help for people who find typing slow or difficult.

## How to use voice typing

1. Choose the **language** you will speak.
2. Press **Start dictation** and allow the microphone when your browser asks.
3. Speak clearly. Words appear in gray while they are being recognized and are then added to the text.
4. Press **Stop dictation** when you are done.
5. Edit the text if needed, then **Copy**, **Download** or **Clear** it.

Your text is kept in your browser if you reload the page, so you won't lose a long dictation.

## Tips for accurate dictation

- **Use a good microphone.** A headset or earbuds with a microphone near your mouth work far better than a laptop microphone across the room.
- **Speak in full sentences** at a steady pace, as if dictating to a person.
- **Reduce background noise:** turn off music and close windows.
- **Pick the right language and region,** for example English (India) or English (UK), so accents and spellings match.
- **Say punctuation** where supported: "comma", "period" or "full stop", "question mark", "new paragraph".
- **Proofread.** Names, technical terms and homophones such as "there" and "their" often need a quick fix.

## Great uses for dictation

- Drafting emails and messages without typing on a small phone keyboard.
- Capturing ideas and meeting notes quickly.
- Writing first drafts of essays and articles, then polishing them by hand.
- Accessibility for people with RSI, arthritis, dyslexia or limited mobility.

## Troubleshooting

- **Nothing happens:** check that the microphone is allowed for this site and that the right microphone is selected in your system settings. Our [Mic and Webcam Test](/mic-webcam-test) helps.
- **"Network" error:** Chrome and Edge need an internet connection for recognition.
