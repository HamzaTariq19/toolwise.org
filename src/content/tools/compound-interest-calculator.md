## What this calculator does

The compound interest calculator shows how money grows over time when interest is added to your balance and then earns interest itself. Enter a starting amount, how much you will add each month, an annual interest rate and the number of years. You instantly see your future balance, how much of it came from your own deposits, and how much is interest. The chart and year-by-year table show the growth curve clearly.

## How to use it

1. Enter your **starting amount**. Use 0 if you are starting from nothing.
2. Enter your **monthly contribution**, the amount you plan to add each month.
3. Enter the expected **annual interest rate**.
4. Enter the number of **years** you will save or invest.
5. Choose how often interest is **compounded**. Monthly is typical for savings accounts.

## The power of time

The most important number in compound interest is time. Consider saving 200 a month at 7% a year:

- After **10 years**, you will have deposited 24,000 and have about 34,600.
- After **20 years**, you will have deposited 48,000 and have about 104,000.
- After **30 years**, you will have deposited 72,000 and have about 244,000.

In the later years, the interest earned each year can exceed the amount you deposit. That is compounding at work, and it is why starting even a few years earlier can make a large difference.

## The formula

For a single deposit, the future value is **A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n × t)**, where P is the starting amount, r is the annual rate as a decimal, n is the number of times interest is compounded per year and t is the number of years. With monthly contributions, toolwise simulates every month: it applies that month's interest, then adds your contribution.

## Tips for growing your savings

- **Automate** your monthly contribution so it happens before you can spend it.
- **Increase contributions** whenever your income rises.
- **Watch fees.** A 1% yearly fee can reduce your final balance by a fifth or more over 30 years.
- **Set a target.** Use our [Savings Goal Calculator](/savings-goal-calculator) to work out how much to save each month for a specific goal.

## Important note

Results are estimates based on a constant rate of return. Real investments rise and fall, and past returns do not guarantee future results. This calculator is for education and planning, not financial advice.
