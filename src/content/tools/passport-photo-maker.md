## What this tool does

The passport photo maker crops your photo to the official size for your country and positions your head correctly, ready to print or upload. Choose your country, add a photo, line up your face with the on-screen guide and download. You can save a single digital photo for online applications or a 4 × 6 inch sheet with several copies to print cheaply at any photo kiosk.

## How to make a passport photo

1. Choose your country or photo size from the list.
2. Tap **Choose photo** and select a clear, front-facing picture.
3. Drag the photo and use the **Zoom** slider so your face fits inside the dashed oval. The top of your head should sit between the upper green lines and your chin on the lower green line.
4. Adjust **Brightness** if the photo looks too dark.
5. Press **Download photo** for a digital copy, or **4 × 6 in print sheet** to print.

## Supported sizes

- **United States** passport and visa: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), head 1 to 1⅜ inches tall.
- **United Kingdom, Schengen countries, India, Australia, Japan** and most others: 35 × 45 mm.
- **Canada** passport: 50 × 70 mm.
- **China** visa: 33 × 48 mm.

## Tips for a photo that gets accepted

- **Lighting:** face a window for soft, even daylight. Avoid shadows on your face or on the wall behind you.
- **Background:** a plain white, off-white or light gray wall works for most countries.
- **Expression:** neutral face, mouth closed, eyes open and looking straight at the camera.
- **Glasses:** most countries now ask you to remove them.
- **Camera position:** hold the camera at eye level, not above or below. Using the back camera of a phone gives a sharper image than the selfie camera.
- **Recent photo:** most authorities require a photo taken within the last six months.

## Printing correctly

When you order prints, choose a 4 × 6 inch (10 × 15 cm) print and make sure the service does not crop, zoom or add borders, otherwise the photo size changes. Measure one photo with a ruler after printing to confirm it is correct.

## Your photo stays private

Your photo is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded. If the online form also has a file size limit, run the downloaded photo through [Compress Image to KB](/compress-image-to-kb).
