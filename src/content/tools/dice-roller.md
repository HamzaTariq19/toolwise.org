## What this tool does

The dice roller rolls virtual dice for board games, tabletop role-playing games, classroom activities and anything else that needs a fair random number. Pick a die type and how many to roll, or type standard dice notation such as **3d6**, **d20+5** or **2d8+1d4−1**. You'll see each die, the total, and a list of your recent rolls.

## How to roll

1. Tap a **die type** (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 or d100). It rolls straight away.
2. Change **how many** with the − and + buttons.
3. Or type any combination in the **notation** box.
4. Press **Roll dice**.

Six-sided dice are shown with pips, like real dice. Other dice show their number.

## Dice notation explained

- **d20** is one twenty-sided die.
- **4d6** is four six-sided dice added together.
- **1d8+2** adds a modifier of 2 to the roll.
- **2d6−1** subtracts 1.
- **1d20+1d4** combines different dice.

## Common dice in games

- **d6:** most board games, like Monopoly, Yahtzee and backgammon.
- **d20:** attacks and checks in Dungeons & Dragons and similar games.
- **d4, d8, d10, d12:** damage rolls and other tabletop mechanics.
- **d100 (percentile):** rolls from 1 to 100, often used for percentage chances.

## Probability with multiple dice

When you add dice together, middle totals are more common than extreme ones. With two six-sided dice, 7 is the most likely total (1 in 6), while 2 and 12 each come up only 1 time in 36. That's why rolling 7 matters so much in many games.
