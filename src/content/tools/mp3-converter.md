## What this tool does

The MP3 converter turns audio files into MP3, the format that plays on every phone, computer, car stereo and media player. Choose several files at once (WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, Opus or WebM), pick a quality, and the converter works through them one by one. Download each MP3 or all of them together as a ZIP file. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

## How to convert to MP3

1. Choose the **quality** (192 kbps is recommended) and whether to keep stereo or make the files **mono**.
2. Choose your audio files or drag them onto the page. Up to 30 files of 100 MB each.
3. Wait while each file is converted. The progress bar shows how far along it is.
4. Download each MP3, or press **Download all (ZIP)**.

## Choosing a bitrate

The bitrate is how much data is used per second of audio:

- **128 kbps:** about 1 MB per minute. Good for speech and casual listening.
- **192 kbps:** about 1.4 MB per minute. Very good quality for most music.
- **256 kbps:** near-transparent for most listeners.
- **320 kbps:** about 2.4 MB per minute. The maximum for MP3.

## When to convert to MP3

- **WAV recordings** from a recorder or audio software are huge; MP3 makes them easy to email and share.
- **M4A voice memos** from an iPhone convert to MP3 for devices and apps that don't play M4A.
- **FLAC music** converts to MP3 for older players and car stereos.
- **OGG or WebM audio** from web downloads and games becomes widely playable.

## Quality tips

Always convert from the best original you have. If you have a WAV or FLAC version, use that instead of an existing MP3, because converting MP3 to MP3 loses a little quality each time.
