## What this calculator does

The retirement calculator projects how much your retirement savings could grow to by the age you plan to stop working. It shows the total in future money and in today's money (after inflation), how much of it comes from your own contributions versus investment growth, and the monthly income your savings might provide using a withdrawal rate such as the popular 4% rule.

## How to use it

1. Enter **your age** and your planned **retirement age**.
2. Enter your **retirement savings today**.
3. Enter how much **you save each month**, including any employer contributions.
4. Set an expected **investment return** and **inflation** rate.
5. Choose a **withdrawal rate** for retirement income, usually 3 to 4%.

## Understanding the results

- **Savings at retirement** is the projected balance in the money of that future year.
- **In today's money** removes the effect of inflation so you can compare it with your current costs.
- **Monthly income** is your withdrawal rate applied to the balance, divided by 12.
- **Investment growth** shows how much compounding contributes. Over long periods it often exceeds what you put in.

## How much do I need?

A common rule of thumb is that you'll need around 70 to 80% of your pre-retirement income each year, from all sources combined. Compare the monthly income in today's money with your expected spending, then add any state or workplace pensions.

## Ways to improve your retirement outlook

- **Start early.** Time is the most powerful factor in compound growth.
- **Capture employer matching.** It is effectively free money.
- **Increase contributions** whenever you get a raise.
- **Keep fees low.** High annual fees quietly reduce your final balance.
- **Review regularly** and adjust if your plans change.

## Important note

This projection assumes a steady return every year, which real markets do not deliver. Taxes, fees, pension rules and personal circumstances also affect the outcome. Use the result to explore scenarios, and consider speaking to a qualified financial adviser for personal advice.
