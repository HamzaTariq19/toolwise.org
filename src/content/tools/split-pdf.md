## When to use it

Split PDF breaks one PDF into several smaller files. Use it when a scanner put a whole stack of documents into one file, when you need to send each person only their own pages (payslips, invoices, certificates), when a long report must be uploaded chapter by chapter, or when a file is too big to email in one go.

## How to split a PDF

1. Tap **Choose PDF file**, or drag a PDF onto the box.
2. Under **How do you want to split it?**, pick a method:
   - **Custom ranges**: type ranges in **Page ranges**, such as `1-3, 4-6, 7`. Each range becomes a separate PDF.
   - **Every page**: each page becomes its own file.
   - **Every N pages**: set **Pages per file**, for example 2. The hint tells you how many files you'll get.
3. Press **Split PDF**.
4. Check the list of new files and their page counts, then press **Download**. Several files arrive as one ZIP named split-pdf.zip.
5. Use **Choose another file** or **Start over** for the next document.

## A worked example

Your office scanned 12 employee payslips into one 12-page file, "payslips-march.pdf", with one payslip per page. Choose **Every page** and press **Split PDF**. You get 12 files named "payslips-march-page-1.pdf" to "payslips-march-page-12.pdf", ready to send to each person. If each payslip were two pages long, you'd choose **Every N pages** with 2, giving six files like "payslips-march-pages-1-2.pdf".

## Tips

- **Check page numbers first** in your PDF reader; the numbers printed on the pages may differ from the actual page order.
- **Use Custom ranges for chapters,** for example `1-12, 13-30, 31-48`.
- **Rename files after downloading** if you send them to different people, so names make sense to them.
- **Too big to email?** Split into two or three parts, or try Compress PDF first.

## Common mistakes

- **Typing ranges with "to" or semicolons.** Use hyphens for ranges and commas between files.
- **Ranges past the last page.** If a document has 10 pages, "8-12" can't work; the tool tells you the page count.
- **Using Split when you want a smaller selection.** To keep pages 2, 5 and 9 together in one file, Extract PDF Pages is quicker.
- **Leaving out pages by accident.** With Custom ranges, pages you don't list aren't included in any file.

## Private by design

Your PDF is split inside your browser and never uploaded, which makes the tool safe for payslips, contracts and medical records.
