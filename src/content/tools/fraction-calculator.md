## What this calculator does

The fraction calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides fractions, mixed numbers, whole numbers and decimals. It gives the answer in simplest form, as a mixed number and as a decimal, and shows the working step by step, so it's useful both for checking homework and for learning how fractions work.

## How to use it

1. Type the **first fraction**, such as `3/4`, `1 1/2`, `5` or `0.25`.
2. Choose the **operation**: +, −, × or ÷.
3. Type the **second fraction**.
4. Read the result, the mixed number, the decimal and the steps.

## Examples

- 1 1/2 + 2/3 = **13/6** = 2 1/6 ≈ 2.1667
- 3/4 − 1/3 = **5/12**
- 2/5 × 15/8 = **3/4**
- 1/2 ÷ 3/4 = **2/3**

## The rules in brief

- **Adding and subtracting:** give the fractions the same denominator, then add or subtract the numerators.
- **Multiplying:** multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.
- **Dividing:** multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
- **Simplifying:** divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor.
- **Mixed numbers:** convert to improper fractions first (2 1/3 = 7/3).

## Fractions in everyday life

Fractions appear in recipes, measurements, construction and money. Scaling a recipe by 1 1/2, cutting wood to 5/8 of an inch, or splitting a bill three ways all use the same rules. For cooking conversions, the [Cooking Measurement Converter](/cooking-converter) accepts fractions directly.

## Tips for students

- Check your answer with the decimal: does it make sense?
- Always simplify the final answer unless your teacher asks otherwise.
- If a denominator is 0, the fraction is undefined, and the calculator will tell you.
