## When to use it

Use this calculator to add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions and mixed numbers, and to see the working. It is ideal for checking maths homework, scaling recipes, measuring wood and fabric, or any time you need an exact answer instead of a rounded decimal.

## How to use it

1. Type the **First fraction**, such as 1 1/2.
2. Choose the **Operation**: +, −, × or ÷.
3. Type the **Second fraction**, such as 2/3.
4. Read the **Result** as a simplified fraction, the **Mixed number** and the **Decimal**.
5. Follow the **Steps** to see how the answer was worked out.

## Worked example

Add 1 1/2 and 2/3. First, the tool writes the mixed number as a fraction: 1 1/2 becomes 3/2. Then it finds a common denominator of 6, turning the fractions into 9/6 and 4/6. Adding the tops gives 13/6. As a mixed number that is 2 1/6, and as a decimal about 2.1667.

For a recipe, you might need 2/3 of a 1 1/2 cup measure. Choose × and the result is 1, exactly one cup.

## How each operation works

- **Adding and subtracting:** make the bottoms (denominators) the same, then add or subtract the tops.
- **Multiplying:** multiply the tops together and the bottoms together.
- **Dividing:** flip the second fraction and multiply.
- **Simplifying:** divide the top and bottom by their largest common factor.

## Fractions in everyday life

Carpenters measure in sixteenths of an inch, cooks in halves and thirds of a cup, and musicians count beats in quarters and eighths. Working in fractions avoids the small rounding errors that creep in when you convert to decimals and back.

## Tips

- **Use the steps to learn.** Cover the answer and try each step yourself, then check.
- **Use fractions for exact work.** Decimals like 0.333 are rounded, but 1/3 is exact.
- **Convert decimals quickly.** Type 0.75 as the first value and add 0 to see it as 3/4.

## Common mistakes

- **Adding the bottoms.** 1/2 + 1/3 is not 2/5. Find a common denominator first: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
- **Forgetting to turn mixed numbers into fractions.** Always rewrite 1 1/2 as 3/2 before multiplying or dividing.
- **Leaving answers unsimplified.** 6/8 should be written as 3/4.
- **Missing the space in mixed numbers.** 11/2 means eleven halves, while 1 1/2 means one and a half.
