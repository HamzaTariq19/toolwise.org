## What this tool does

The black and white converter removes color from your photos. Choose classic **Grayscale** for timeless portraits and landscapes, **Black & white** for crisp two-tone images of documents and signatures, or **Sepia** for a warm vintage look. A live preview shows every change instantly.

## How to convert a photo to black and white

1. Tap **Choose image** and pick a photo.
2. Choose a **Style**: Grayscale, Black & white or Sepia.
3. For Grayscale and Sepia, adjust **Contrast**. For Black & white, adjust **Threshold**.
4. Press **Download image**.

## When to use each style

- **Grayscale** brings out shape, texture and light. It works beautifully for portraits, street photography and dramatic skies, and can rescue photos taken under ugly mixed lighting.
- **Black & white** produces a clean, high-contrast image with no grays. Use it for scanned pages, receipts, handwritten notes, signatures, QR-style graphics and stencils for crafts.
- **Sepia** adds warm brown tones reminiscent of old photographs, great for family history projects and vintage-style invitations.

## Tips for striking black and white photos

- **Boost contrast** slightly for punchier images; many photos look flat when color is removed.
- **Look for strong light and shadow.** Side lighting and backlighting translate especially well.
- **Simplify the scene.** Without color, busy backgrounds become more distracting; crop them away with [Crop Image](/crop-image).
- **For documents,** start with a threshold around the middle and move it until text is solid and the background clean.

## Using it for forms and documents

Many application portals ask for signatures or documents as clear black-and-white images within a size limit. Convert your photo with Black & white here, then use [Compress Image to KB](/compress-image-to-kb) to meet the limit.
