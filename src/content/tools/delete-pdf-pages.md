## When to use it

Delete PDF Pages removes pages you don't need from a PDF. Use it to drop blank pages from a scan, take out a cover sheet or terms pages before sending a document, remove pages with private information before sharing, or cut a long report down to the parts that matter. Smaller, focused documents are quicker to read and easier to email.

## How to delete pages from a PDF

1. Tap **Choose PDF file**, or drag a PDF onto the box.
2. Wait for the page thumbnails to appear.
3. **Tap the pages you want to remove.** They're marked with a red cross. Tap again to unmark one, or press **Clear selection** to start over.
4. Or type the page numbers in **Or type the pages to delete**, such as `1-3, 5`, and press **Select**.
5. Press **Delete pages**. The button shows how many pages will go.
6. Press **Download** to save the new file, which ends in "-edited.pdf".

## A worked example

Your bank statement PDF has 9 pages: the statement on pages 1 to 3, then six pages of standard terms and adverts. A landlord only needs the statement. Open the file, type `4-9` and press **Select**: pages 4 to 9 get red crosses. Press **Delete pages** and download a 3-page PDF that's smaller, clearer, and doesn't share more than needed.

## Tips

- **Zoom in on thumbnails** in your browser if you need to read them before deciding.
- **Use typed ranges for long documents;** tapping 40 pages one by one is slow.
- **Check page numbers** against the thumbnails: the numbers printed on pages can differ from their position in the file.
- **Blank pages from double-sided scanning** are easy to spot as white thumbnails.

## Delete or extract?

If you're removing a few pages from a long document, use this tool. If you're keeping only a few pages, use Extract PDF Pages instead and pick the ones to keep; it's the same result with fewer taps.

## Common mistakes

- **Typing ranges with words** like "1 to 3"; use `1-3`.
- **Relying on deletion to hide sensitive information.** Deleted pages are gone, but if the information also appears on pages you keep, it stays visible.
- **Forgetting about the original.** The download is a new file; delete or replace the old one yourself if needed.
- **Trying to delete every page.** Keep at least one.

## Private by design

Pages are removed in your browser, so confidential documents never leave your device.
