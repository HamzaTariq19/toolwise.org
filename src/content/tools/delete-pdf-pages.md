## What this tool does

Delete PDF Pages removes the pages you don't need and saves the rest as a new PDF. Use it to drop blank pages from a scan, remove an outdated section from a report, take advertising pages out of a downloaded document, or strip a cover sheet before sending a file to someone else.

## How to delete pages from a PDF

1. Tap **Choose PDF file** and select your document.
2. Tap each page you want to remove. A red cross shows it will be deleted, and the preview fades.
3. Alternatively, type page numbers like `1, 4-6` in the box and press **Select**.
4. Press **Delete pages**. The button shows exactly how many pages will go.
5. Download your shorter PDF.

## Tips for cleaning up documents

- **Blank pages from double-sided scans** are easy to spot in the previews because they are white. Tap them all, then delete in one go.
- **Long documents:** typing a range such as `20-45` is quicker than tapping many pages.
- **Want to keep only a few pages instead?** Use [Extract PDF Pages](/extract-pdf-pages); it is the same job approached from the other side.
- **Pages in the wrong order?** Fix that with [Reorder PDF Pages](/reorder-pdf-pages).
- **Still too large to email?** Run the result through [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf).

## What stays the same

The remaining pages are not re-rendered or compressed. Text stays sharp and searchable, images keep their quality, and page sizes and orientations are unchanged. The new file is usually smaller than the original, because the content of deleted pages is removed too.

## Deleting versus redacting

Deleting a page removes it completely, which is a safe way to keep confidential pages out of a document you share. If you need to hide only part of a page, such as an account number, deleting is not enough and you need a proper redaction tool that removes the underlying text.

## Checking before you save

Scroll through the previews once more before pressing the button: marked pages are faded and carry a red cross, so it is easy to spot a page you selected by mistake. Tap it again to keep it. The page count shown at the top of the tool and the number on the button should add up to the length you expect for the final document.

## Your files stay on your device

All the work happens in your browser. The PDF is never sent to a server, so private documents remain private.
