## When to use it

Use this tool to make a professional email signature with your name, job title, company, contact details and social links. A clear signature makes it easy for people to call you, visit your website or find you on LinkedIn, and gives every email a consistent, polished finish.

## How to use it

1. Fill in your **Full name**, **Job title**, **Company**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Website**.
2. Optionally add an **Address**, a **Photo or logo URL**, a **LinkedIn URL** and an **X / Instagram URL**.
3. Choose a **Layout**: **Photo on the left**, **Stacked** or **Compact line**.
4. Pick an **Accent color** to match your brand.
5. Check the **Preview**, then click **Copy signature** or **Copy HTML**.
6. Open **How to add it to Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail** for step-by-step instructions.

## Worked example

Jordan Rivera is a Marketing Manager at Brightside Studio. With name, title, company, phone, email and website filled in, and a logo hosted on the company website, Jordan chooses **Photo on the left** and the studio's orange as the accent color.

To add it to Gmail, Jordan clicks **Copy signature**, then goes to Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new, pastes it and saves. In Outlook on the web, it is Settings → Account → Signatures → New signature. In Apple Mail, it is Mail → Settings → Signatures → +, untick "Always match my default message font", then paste.

## Signatures for a whole team

If several people at your company need matching signatures, fill in the details for each person with the same layout and accent color. Everyone's emails then look consistent, which helps your brand look organised.

## Tips

- **Keep it short.** Name, title, company, one phone number and a website are usually enough.
- **Use a small logo or photo.** Around 100 pixels wide is plenty and loads quickly.
- **Send yourself a test email.** Check it on your phone as well as your computer.
- **Match your brand color.** Use your logo's main color as the accent.

## Common mistakes

- **Attaching the logo as a file.** It shows up as an attachment in every email. Use an online image URL instead.
- **Adding quotes, banners and many icons.** Long signatures distract from your message and may land in spam filters.
- **Forgetting the country code.** Write your phone number with + and the country code, such as +1, so people abroad can call.
- **Pasting into the wrong place.** Paste into the signature box in your email settings, not into a new email.
