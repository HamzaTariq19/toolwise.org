## What this tool does

The email signature generator creates a clean, professional signature for your emails. Add your name, job title, company, phone, email, website, address, a photo or logo and social links, choose a layout and accent color, and copy the finished signature straight into Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail or another email app.

## How to make your signature

1. Fill in your details. Empty optional fields are left out.
2. For a photo or logo, enter the URL of an image hosted online.
3. Choose a **layout** and **accent color**.
4. Check the preview.
5. Press **Copy signature** and paste it into your email app's signature settings. Use **Copy HTML** for apps that ask for code.

## What to include

- **Must have:** full name, job title, company and one main contact method.
- **Nice to have:** website, phone, a small photo or logo, one or two social profiles.
- **Leave out:** long quotes, multiple phone numbers, large images and many social icons.

## Design tips

- **Keep it short:** four to six lines is plenty.
- **One accent color** matching your brand looks professional.
- **Small images:** around 80 pixels wide keeps emails light.
- **Make links clickable** so people can call or visit your site with one tap.
- **Test it** by sending an email to yourself and opening it on your phone.

## Adding it to other apps

- **Outlook:** Settings → Account → Signatures → New signature → paste.
- **Apple Mail:** Settings → Signatures → + → paste, and untick "Always match my default message font".
