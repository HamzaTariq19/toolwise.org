## What this tool does

The barcode generator creates scannable barcodes in the most common formats: EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8, Code 128, Code 39, ITF-14 and Codabar. It validates your input, adds check digits automatically, and lets you download the barcode as a high-resolution PNG or a vector SVG.

## How to create a barcode

1. Choose the **barcode type**.
2. Type the **content**. The hint explains what that type accepts.
3. Adjust the **height** and **bar width**, and choose whether to show the text.
4. Scan the preview with your phone to check it.
5. Download a **PNG** or **SVG**.

## Barcode types explained

- **EAN-13:** the standard retail barcode outside North America; 13 digits.
- **UPC-A:** the standard retail barcode in the US and Canada; 12 digits.
- **EAN-8:** a short version for small packages.
- **Code 128:** compact and flexible; letters, numbers and symbols. Great for asset tags and shipping labels.
- **Code 39:** an older format for uppercase letters and digits, still used in automotive and defense.
- **ITF-14:** printed on outer cartons and cases.
- **Codabar:** used by libraries, blood banks and some logistics systems.

## Printing tips

- **Size:** don't shrink retail barcodes too much; around 37 × 26 mm is a common EAN-13 size.
- **Quiet zone:** leave blank space on both sides of the bars.
- **Contrast:** print black bars on a white background. Red bars are invisible to many scanners.
- **Test:** print a sample and scan it before producing labels in bulk.
