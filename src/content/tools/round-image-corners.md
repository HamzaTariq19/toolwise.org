## When to use it

Round Image Corners gives pictures soft rounded corners or turns them into circles. Use it for profile pictures and team photos in a circle, rounded product shots and screenshots for a website or presentation, app-icon style graphics, and photos in email signatures, where rounding with code often doesn't work.

## How to round image corners

1. Tap **Choose image**, or drag a JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC file onto the box.
2. Choose a **Shape**: **Rounded** or **Circle**.
3. For Rounded, set the **Corner size** (for example 12%).
4. Choose **Save as**. PNG keeps the corners transparent; JPG fills them with white.
5. Press **Download image**.

## A worked example

You're adding headshots to an "About us" slide and want them to look consistent. For each photo, crop it to a square with the face centred, then open it here and choose **Circle**. Save as **PNG** so the corners are transparent over the slide's coloured background. All five headshots now appear as matching circles, without white boxes around them.

## Tips

- **Check what's in the centre** before choosing Circle: wide or tall images are trimmed to a centred square, so crop first if the subject is off to one side.
- **Use PNG for transparent corners** on coloured backgrounds, websites and slides.
- **Use JPG with white corners** when the image will sit on a white page anyway; files are smaller.
- **Keep corner sizes consistent** across a set of images for a polished look.
- **Leave space around faces** when making circles, so heads aren't clipped.

## How the shapes are made

**Rounded** keeps the whole picture and trims each corner along a curve. **Circle** keeps the largest centred square and cuts a circle from it, so the output is always square, with transparent (or white) corners outside the circle.

## Where rounded images work well

- **Profile and team photos** as circles.
- **Product cards and thumbnails** with a small radius.
- **Screenshots in tutorials,** which look tidier with 2 to 4% corners.
- **Email signatures,** where rounded photos look more personal.

## Common mistakes

- **Saving as JPG on a coloured background,** leaving visible white corners.
- **Making a circle from a wide group photo,** which keeps only the middle.
- **Very large corner sizes on wide images,** which can look like a pill shape.

## Private by design

Your image is edited in your browser and never uploaded.
