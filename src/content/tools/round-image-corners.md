## What this tool does

Round Image Corners gives a picture soft rounded corners or turns it into a circle. Rounded images look modern and friendly in presentations, websites, documents, app mockups and social posts, and circles are the standard shape for profile pictures and team photos.

## How to round the corners of an image

1. Tap **Choose image** and select your picture.
2. Choose **Rounded** or **Circle**.
3. For rounded corners, drag **Corner size** until it looks right in the preview.
4. Keep **PNG** selected for transparent corners.
5. Press **Download image**.

## Ideas for rounded images

- **Team pages and author bios** with circular headshots.
- **Presentation slides** where rounded photos match rounded shapes and cards.
- **Product images** that echo the rounded style of modern websites.
- **App and game mockups,** where icons use rounded square shapes.
- **Documents and newsletters** that need a softer, friendlier layout.

## Tips

- **Crop first for circles.** The circle uses the center of the image, so crop to a square with [Crop Image](/crop-image) and put the face slightly above the center.
- **Match your design system.** If your website uses 12-pixel rounded cards, choose a corner size that gives a similar curve at the size the image will be displayed.
- **Check on dark backgrounds.** Transparent corners look clean anywhere, but light edges of a photo can stand out on dark pages; a thin border in your design can help.
- **Profile picture upload?** Many platforms crop images to circles themselves. Upload a square image in that case, and use this tool when you control how the image is displayed.

## About transparency

Transparent PNG images can be placed on top of any color. If a website or app shows the corners as black or white, it does not support transparency; in that case save as JPG to get white corners.

## Private

Your image is processed in your browser and never uploaded.
