## What this converter does

The speed converter changes a speed between the units used on roads, in sport, at sea, in the air and in science: kilometers per hour, miles per hour, meters per second, knots, feet per second and Mach.

## How to use it

1. Type the **speed**.
2. Choose the unit you have and the unit you want.
3. Read the result and the table of all other units.

## Common speed conversions

- 1 mph ≈ 1.609 km/h
- 1 km/h ≈ 0.621 mph ≈ 0.278 m/s
- 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h ≈ 2.237 mph
- 1 knot ≈ 1.852 km/h ≈ 1.151 mph

## Speed limits and driving

- 30 mph ≈ 48 km/h
- 50 km/h ≈ 31 mph
- 70 mph ≈ 113 km/h
- 100 km/h ≈ 62 mph
- 130 km/h ≈ 81 mph

When driving abroad, check whether road signs and your speedometer use km/h or mph.

## Running and cycling

Running pace is often given in minutes per kilometer or mile rather than speed. To get speed, divide 60 by your pace: a 5:00 per km pace is 12 km/h, or about 7.5 mph.

## Weather and wind

Wind speed may be reported in km/h, mph, m/s or knots depending on the country and service. 20 knots is about 37 km/h or 23 mph, a fresh breeze strong enough to make small branches sway.
