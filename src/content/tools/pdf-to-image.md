## What this tool does

PDF to Image turns each page of a PDF into a picture file. That is handy when you want to post a page on social media, put a slide into a presentation or chat message, use a page as a thumbnail, or send a document to someone whose device struggles with PDFs. You can choose JPG or PNG, pick the resolution, and convert all pages or only the ones you need.

## How to convert a PDF to images

1. Tap **Choose PDF file** or drag a PDF onto the box.
2. Choose **JPG** or **PNG**.
3. Choose the **image quality**. Normal is right for most uses.
4. Optionally type the pages you want, like `1-3, 5`. Leave it empty for all pages.
5. Press **Convert to images**, then download the images one by one or all together as a ZIP.

## Understanding DPI

DPI means "dots per inch" and decides how many pixels each page gets. A standard A4 or Letter page becomes:

- about **600 × 840 pixels** at 72 DPI,
- about **1240 × 1750 pixels** at 150 DPI,
- about **2480 × 3500 pixels** at 300 DPI.

Higher DPI means sharper images but larger files. For very large pages, such as posters, toolwise automatically limits the size so the conversion works reliably on phones.

## Tips

- To get a crisp image of a chart or table, choose PNG at High quality.
- If you need the images even smaller afterwards, use [Compress Image](/compress-image).
- To go the other way and turn pictures into a PDF, use [Image to PDF](/image-to-pdf).
- To copy the text of a PDF instead of taking a picture of it, use [PDF to Text](/pdf-to-text).

Many converters upload your document to their servers. toolwise renders the pages directly on your device, so your PDF stays private and the conversion starts immediately, without waiting for an upload.
