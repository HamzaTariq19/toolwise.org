## What this converter does

The length converter changes a measurement from one unit of length or distance to another. Enter a value, choose the unit you have and the unit you want, and the answer appears instantly. Every other unit is listed underneath, so you can see the same length in metric and imperial at a glance.

## How to use it

1. Type the **value**.
2. Choose the **unit you have** in the left list.
3. Choose the **unit you want** in the right list.
4. Read the result, or look at the **All conversions** table. Use the swap button to reverse the direction.

## Common length conversions

- 1 inch = 2.54 cm
- 1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 cm
- 1 yard = 3 feet = 0.9144 m
- 1 mile = 1,760 yards = 1.609344 km
- 1 meter ≈ 3.281 feet ≈ 39.37 inches
- 1 kilometer ≈ 0.621 miles

- **DIY and furniture:** check whether a sofa listed in inches fits a space measured in centimeters.
- **Science and school:** move between millimeters, micrometers and meters.

## Metric and imperial

The metric system is based on powers of ten, so converting between millimeters, centimeters, meters and kilometers just moves the decimal point. Imperial units such as inches, feet, yards and miles use different multiples (12, 3, 1,760), which is where a converter is most helpful.
