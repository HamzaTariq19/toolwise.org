## What this calculator does

The credit card payoff calculator shows how long it will take to clear a credit card balance and how much interest you will pay along the way. You can work it either way round: enter a fixed monthly payment to see your debt-free date, or choose a number of months to see the payment you need.

## How to use it

1. Enter your **card balance** and the card's **interest rate (APR)**, found on your statement.
2. Choose **Pay a fixed amount** and enter your monthly payment, or **Be debt-free by** and enter the number of months.
3. See the time to pay off, total interest, total paid and your debt-free date.
4. Read the tip below the results to see how much faster an extra 25% a month would get you there.

## Example

A balance of 5,000 at 22.9% APR, paid at 250 a month, takes about 2 years and 2 months to clear and costs roughly 1,360 in interest. Raising the payment to 312.50 clears it about 6 months sooner and saves several hundred in interest.

## Strategies to pay off credit cards faster

- **Pay more than the minimum,** even a small amount. Every extra payment reduces the balance that interest is charged on.
- **Stop adding new purchases** to the card while you pay it down.
- **Pay on time** to avoid late fees and penalty interest rates.
- **Consider a 0% balance transfer** or a lower-rate consolidation loan.
- **Ask your card provider for a lower rate,** especially if you have a good payment history.
- **Several cards?** Use the [Debt Snowball Calculator](/debt-snowball-calculator) to decide which to pay first.

## How the calculation works

Each month, interest is added based on the APR divided by 12, then your payment is subtracted. The calculator repeats this until the balance reaches zero. Real cards calculate interest on daily balances and may have fees, so your actual figures may differ slightly.
