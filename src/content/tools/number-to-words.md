## What this converter does

The number to words converter writes any number out in English words. It's useful for writing checks, legal and financial documents, invoices, wedding invitations, and for teaching children how to read large numbers. It handles decimals, negative numbers, ordinal numbers like "twenty-first" and currency amounts.

## How to use it

1. Type the **number**. Commas and spaces are fine.
2. Choose how to **write it**: words, check amount, ordinal or digit by digit.
3. For checks, pick the **currency**.
4. Choose British-style **"and"** and **capitalization** if you need them.
5. Press **Copy**.

## Examples

- 1,234 → one thousand two hundred thirty-four
- 105 (British) → one hundred and five
- 21 (ordinal) → twenty-first
- 3.75 → three and seventy-five hundredths
- 1,250.50 dollars → one thousand two hundred fifty dollars and fifty cents

## Writing numbers in documents

- **Checks:** write the amount in words exactly, then draw a line to fill any remaining space so nothing can be added.
- **Contracts and invoices:** many documents state amounts in both figures and words, such as "USD 5,000 (five thousand US dollars)". If they differ, the words usually take precedence.
- **Style guides:** many recommend spelling out numbers from one to nine in normal text and using figures for 10 and above.

## Large number names

- Thousand: 1,000
- Million: 1,000,000
- Billion: 1,000,000,000
- Trillion: 1,000,000,000,000

Beyond that come quadrillion, quintillion, sextillion and so on.
