## What this tool does

This converter turns JPG (also called JPEG) images into PNG files. PNG is a lossless format, which means the image is stored exactly, pixel for pixel. That makes it the better choice when you are going to edit an image several times, add text or graphics, or upload it to a service that requires PNG.

## How to convert JPG to PNG

1. Tap **Choose images** and select one or more JPG files, or drag them onto the box.
2. Each image is converted straight away. A progress bar shows the progress for larger batches.
3. Check the list of converted files and their dimensions.
4. Press **Download**. Several files come together as a ZIP.

## When PNG is the right choice

- **Editing in steps:** each time a JPG is saved it loses a little quality. Working in PNG avoids that build-up.
- **Graphics with text:** screenshots, diagrams and images with lettering look crisper as PNG.
- **Design and print workflows** that ask for lossless files.
- **Websites and apps that only accept PNG,** such as some icon, sticker or avatar uploads.

## When to stay with JPG

For ordinary photos you are sharing or posting online, JPG is usually better because the files are much smaller and the difference is invisible. If file size matters, compare both, or keep the JPG and use [Compress Image](/compress-image) instead.

## About file sizes

Expect the PNG to be noticeably larger than the JPG for photos with lots of detail, such as landscapes or busy scenes. Simple images, like logos or flat-color graphics saved as JPG, grow less. If the PNG ends up too large, our [Compress Image](/compress-image) tool can reduce PNG files using a smaller color palette while keeping them in PNG format.

## Orientation and colors

Phone photos often store their rotation separately from the pixels. toolwise applies that rotation while converting, so your PNG appears the right way up in every app. Colors are kept as they appear on your screen.
