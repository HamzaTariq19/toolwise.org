## When to use it

Extract PDF Pages copies the pages you choose into a new, smaller PDF. Use it to send someone only the pages they need (a signed page, an invoice from a combined file, one chapter), to save the important part of a long manual, or to separate odd and even pages of a double-sided scan. Choosing what to keep is often quicker than deleting everything else.

## How to extract pages from a PDF

1. Tap **Choose PDF file**, or drag a PDF onto the box.
2. Wait for the page thumbnails to appear.
3. **Tap the pages you want to keep.** Or use the quick buttons: **Select all**, **Odd pages**, **Even pages** or **Clear**.
4. You can also type the pages in **Or type the pages to extract**, such as `1-3, 5`, and press **Select**.
5. Press **Extract pages**. They're copied into a new PDF in their original order.
6. Press **Download**. The new file ends in "-extracted.pdf".

## A worked example

A 60-page employee handbook PDF includes the expenses policy on pages 23 to 26 and the claim form on page 58. A new colleague only needs those. Type `23-26, 58` and press **Select**, check that the five right thumbnails are highlighted, then press **Extract pages**. You download a 5-page PDF that's easy to email and read on a phone.

## Ideas

- **Job applications:** pull the relevant certificates out of a combined scan.
- **Studying:** keep the chapters you're revising from a long textbook PDF.
- **Accounts:** take a single month's invoice from a year-long statement file.
- **Double-sided scans:** extract odd pages to one file and even pages to another, then merge them in the right order.

## Tips

- **Use typed ranges for big documents,** then glance at the highlighted thumbnails to check.
- **Rename the download** to describe what's inside, such as "expenses-policy.pdf".
- **Need them in a different order?** Run the result through Reorder PDF Pages.
- **Large result?** Compress it before emailing.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting your selection order to be kept.** Pages always stay in document order.
- **Selecting nothing.** Pick at least one page; the tool tells you if you forgot.
- **Confusing printed page numbers with page positions.** A document's page "1" may be the third page of the file.
- **Extracting from a locked PDF.** Unlock it first.

## Private by design

The new PDF is created in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
