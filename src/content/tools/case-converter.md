## What this tool does

The case converter changes the capitalization of any text instantly. Fix text accidentally typed with Caps Lock on, turn a shouty email into normal sentences, format headlines consistently, or convert phrases into programming styles like camelCase and snake_case.

## How to change text case

1. Type or paste your text into **Your text**.
2. Choose a style under **Convert to**.
3. The converted text appears in **Result** immediately. Switch styles as often as you like.
4. Press **Copy result** or **Download .txt**.

## The styles explained

- **Sentence case:** Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalized. Best for normal writing and most interface text.
- **lowercase:** every letter small. Useful for usernames, tags and email addresses.
- **UPPERCASE:** every letter capital. Use sparingly; in emails and messages it reads as shouting.
- **Title Case:** headline style, Like This for Articles and Book Titles.
- **Capitalize Each Word:** Every Single Word Starts With A Capital.
- **aLtErNaTiNg and iNVERSE:** playful styles for memes and social posts.
- **camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case:** programming and URL styles that also remove punctuation.

## Common fixes

- **Caps Lock accidents:** paste the text and choose Sentence case, then fix names.
- **Inconsistent headings:** run every heading in a document through Title Case for a polished look.
- **Spreadsheet data:** clean a column of names or cities typed in mixed case.
- **Code:** turn a description like "user first name" into `userFirstName` or `user_first_name`.

## Title case rules

Style guides differ slightly. toolwise follows the common headline convention: capitalize all major words, keep articles, short conjunctions and short prepositions in lowercase unless they begin or end the title. If your publication follows a specific guide such as AP or Chicago, double-check words like "with", "from" and "over".
