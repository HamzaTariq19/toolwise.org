## What this tool does

The hashtag generator turns a description of your post, or a list of keywords, into clean, readable hashtags. It combines related words into phrases, removes filler words, formats tags in CamelCase or lowercase, and suggests a sensible number for the platform you're posting on.

## How to use it

1. Describe your post or list keywords. Separate ideas with commas.
2. Choose the **platform** and the **style**.
3. Tap tags to remove or add them. The count updates.
4. Press **Copy selected** and paste into your post.

## Example

"homemade sourdough bread, weekend baking in London" produces tags such as #HomemadeSourdoughBread, #SourdoughBread, #WeekendBaking, #Sourdough and #London.

## Hashtag strategy

- **Relevance beats volume.** Tags should describe your content, not just be popular.
- **Mix sizes:** combine a couple of broad tags with specific, niche ones.
- **Add place tags** for local businesses and events.
- **Create a branded tag** for your business or campaign and use it consistently.
- **Avoid banned or spammy tags,** which can reduce your reach.

## Platform tips

- **Instagram:** tags in the caption or first comment work the same.
- **TikTok:** tags help categorize your video; keep them on topic.
- **LinkedIn:** a few professional tags at the end of a post.
- **X:** one or two tags inside the sentence look natural.
- **YouTube:** tags in the description; the first three appear above the title.
