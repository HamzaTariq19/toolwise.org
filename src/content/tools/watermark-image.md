## What this tool does

Add Watermark to Image places your name, website or logo on a photo so people know who made it. Watermarks discourage others from reposting your work without credit, help potential customers find you when images are shared, and mark proofs and previews as not for final use.

## How to watermark a photo

1. Tap **Choose image** and select your photo.
2. Choose **Text** and type your watermark, or choose **Logo image** and pick your logo file.
3. Tap a **Position** in the grid, or tick **Repeat across the whole image**.
4. Adjust **Size** and **Opacity**. For text, choose a **color**.
5. Press **Download image**.

## Choosing the right settings

- **Opacity:** 40 to 60% is visible without dominating the picture. Proofs for clients can go higher.
- **Size:** around 10 to 15% of the image width is common for corner watermarks.
- **Color:** white works on most photos thanks to a soft shadow around the letters. Use a dark color on very bright images.
- **Repeat across the image:** best for previews of work you are selling, such as photography, artwork or designs.

## Tips for photographers and sellers

- **Be consistent.** Use the same watermark style on all your photos so people recognize your work.
- **Include something searchable,** like your website or social handle, rather than just a symbol.
- **Keep originals unmarked.** Watermark copies for sharing, not your master files.
- **Proofs:** send clients repeated, semi-transparent watermarks, then deliver clean images after payment.

## Text symbols

The © symbol is easy to add: copy it from here (©) and paste it into the text box. In most countries you own the copyright to your photos automatically, but a visible notice makes that clear.
