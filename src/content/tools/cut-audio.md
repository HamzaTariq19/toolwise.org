## When to use it

Use this tool to keep just the part of an audio file you need. It is ideal for making a ringtone, clipping a quote from an interview, trimming silence from the start of a voice memo, or cutting a song down for a video or presentation. You can also add a smooth fade in or fade out.

## How to use it

1. Click **Choose audio** or drop a file. MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC and more work, up to 100 MB.
2. The waveform appears. Click anywhere on it to move the playback position.
3. Set the **Start** and **End** times. Type them in, or play the audio and use the buttons next to each box to set them to the current position. Use **Play selection** to check the cut.
4. Choose a **Fade in** and **Fade out**, from none up to 3 seconds.
5. Under **Save as**, choose **MP3** or **WAV**. For MP3, pick the **MP3 quality**: 128, 192 or 320 kbps.
6. Click **Cut audio**, listen to the result, then click **Download**.

If the cut is not quite right, click **Change the cut** to adjust it without loading the file again.

## Worked example

You want a 30-second ringtone from a song you own. Load the file and play it until the chorus starts at about 1:12. Set the start there, then set the end at 1:42. Add a 1-second fade in and a 2-second fade out so it does not start or stop suddenly. Save as MP3 at 192 kbps. The result is about 0.7 MB, small enough for any phone.

## Tips

- **Use the waveform to find gaps.** Quiet parts look flat, which makes it easy to find the space between sentences or songs.
- **Fine-tune with Play selection.** Listen to the start and end a few times and nudge the times by a fraction of a second.
- **Fades hide rough edges.** Even a 0.5-second fade stops a click when the cut lands in the middle of a sound.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting the cut too tight.** Leave a fraction of a second before the first word so it is not clipped.
- **Choosing WAV for sharing.** WAV files are around seven times larger than 192 kbps MP3s. Use MP3 unless you need full quality.
- **Using audio you do not have rights to.** Only cut and share recordings you own or have permission to use.
