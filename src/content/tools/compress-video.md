## What this tool does

Compress Video makes video files smaller so they're easier to email, share in messaging apps, upload or store. Choose how strongly to compress, optionally lower the resolution, and decide what to do with the sound. The tool re-encodes your video as an MP4 using H.264, the most compatible video format, and shows how much space you saved. It runs entirely in your browser, with no upload and no watermark.

## How to compress a video

1. Choose a video, or drag it onto the page. Files up to 1 GB.
2. Pick a compression level:
   - **Smallest file** for the biggest savings.
   - **Balanced** for a good mix of size and quality.
   - **High quality** when detail matters most.
3. Choose a **resolution**. 720p is a great choice for sharing.
4. Keep, reduce or remove the **sound**.
5. Press **Compress video**, and keep the tab open while it works.
6. Check the result and press **Download**.

## What makes the biggest difference

- **Resolution:** going from 4K or 1080p to 720p usually cuts the size dramatically, and most phone screens can't show the difference.
- **Compression level:** "Smallest file" uses stronger compression, which can make fast movement look softer.
- **Sound:** removing sound saves a little, which helps for silent screen recordings.
- **Length:** for very long videos, consider using [Trim Video](/trim-video) first to keep only what you need.

## If the result isn't smaller

Some videos are already heavily compressed, for example those downloaded from social media. If the result is larger, toolwise tells you so you can keep the original or try stronger settings.
