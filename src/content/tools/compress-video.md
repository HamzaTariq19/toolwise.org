## When to use it

Use this tool when a video is too large to email, upload or send in a chat app. Phone videos are often recorded at very high quality, so a short clip can be hundreds of megabytes. Compressing lowers the file size while keeping the video clear enough to watch.

## How to use it

1. Click **Choose video** or drop a file. MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and more work, up to 1 GB.
2. Wait while the video tools load and the file is read. You will see its size, length and resolution.
3. Choose the **Compression**: **Smallest file**, **Balanced** or **High quality**.
4. Choose the **Resolution**. You can keep the original or pick a lower one such as 1080p, 720p, 480p or 360p. For large videos, 720p is selected for you.
5. Choose the **Sound**: **Keep (good quality)**, **Lower quality, smaller** (mono) or **Remove the sound**.
6. Read the time estimate, then click **Compress video**.
7. When it finishes, the result shows the old and new size and how much smaller it is. Click **Download**, or **Try other settings**.

## Worked example

You have a 2-minute 4K video from your phone that is about 700 MB, and your email allows 25 MB. Choose **Smallest file** and 480p. The tool estimates how long it will take, often a few minutes on a laptop. The compressed video is far smaller and fine for watching on a phone.

For a family video you want to keep looking good on a TV, choose **High quality** and 1080p instead. The file will be larger, but much smaller than the original.

## Tips

- **Lower the resolution first.** Going from 4K to 1080p or 720p cuts the size more than any other setting.
- **Trim before you compress.** Cutting unwanted parts with [Trim Video](/trim-video) makes the job faster and the file smaller.
- **Plug in your laptop.** Compression is hard work for the processor and drains batteries quickly.
- **Keep the tab open.** Switching apps on a phone can pause or stop the work.
- **Remove sound you do not need.** Silent clips for presentations or websites save a little more space.

## Common mistakes

- **Compressing twice.** Each pass loses a little quality. Start from the original file and choose stronger settings instead.
- **Choosing 360p for screen recordings.** Small text becomes hard to read. Keep 720p or higher for screens.
- **Deleting the original too soon.** Check the compressed video before removing the original.
