## What this tool does

Protect PDF adds a password to your document, so only people who know the password can open it. The content is encrypted with AES-256, which means it is scrambled until the correct password is entered. Use it before emailing tax returns, payslips, contracts, medical records or anything else you would not want a stranger to read.

## How to password-protect a PDF

1. Tap **Choose PDF file** and select your PDF.
2. Type a **password** and type it again to confirm. The bar shows how strong it is.
3. Choose what people can do after opening it: **print**, **copy** text and images, and **edit**.
4. Press **Protect PDF** and download the encrypted file.
5. Send the password to the recipient separately, for example by text message or phone, not in the same email as the file.

## Choosing a good password

- **Length beats complexity.** A phrase of four unrelated words, like "lamp river orange cloud", is both strong and easy to share by phone.
- **Avoid personal details** such as birthdays, names or postcodes.
- **Use a different password** for each sensitive document you share with different people.

## Opening the protected file

Any standard PDF reader will ask for the password when the file is opened. After entering it, the document works as usual within the permissions you set. If you later need an unprotected copy and you know the password, use [Unlock PDF](/unlock-pdf).

## About permissions

Permissions are rules stored in the file, such as "printing not allowed". Reputable PDF readers follow them, but some programs ignore them, so do not rely on permissions alone to protect truly sensitive material. The open password is what keeps the content secret. toolwise sets a separate, random permissions password that is never shown, so the rules cannot easily be switched off.

## Protecting several documents

If you send many protected files to the same person, agree on a system in advance, such as a password shared once in person or over the phone, rather than sending a new password with every email. For very sensitive material, combine password protection with a secure file transfer service offered by your bank, employer or accountant.

## Private by design

Encryption happens entirely in your browser. Your PDF and your password are never uploaded or stored, so nobody else, including toolwise, ever has access to them.
