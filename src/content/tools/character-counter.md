## What this tool does

The character counter tells you exactly how long your text is. As you type or paste, it counts characters with and without spaces, letters, words, sentences, lines and bytes. Progress bars show at a glance whether your text fits common limits such as a post on X, an Instagram caption, an SMS, a meta description or a YouTube title.

## How to use it

1. Type or paste your text into the box.
2. Read the counts on the right (below the text on phones).
3. Check the **Common character limits** panel: bars turn amber near a limit and red when you go over, showing exactly how many characters to cut.
4. Use **Copy text** to paste your edited version back where it belongs.

## Character limits worth knowing

- **Posts on X:** 280 characters for standard accounts.
- **SMS text message:** 160 characters for one message using standard letters; fewer if you use emoji or special characters.
- **Meta description:** search engines usually show 150 to 160 characters.
- **Page title:** around 50 to 60 characters before it gets cut off in search results.
- **Instagram and TikTok captions:** 2,200 characters, though only the first line or two show before "more".
- **YouTube video title:** 100 characters, with roughly 60 visible in most layouts.

## Tips for trimming text

- **Lead with the important part.** Many platforms truncate long text, so put the key message first.
- **Cut filler words** like "really", "just", "that" and "in order to".
- **Replace phrases with single words:** "at this point in time" becomes "now".
- **Use numerals** instead of spelling out numbers where the style allows it.
- **Check links:** some platforms shorten links automatically, so they count less than their full length.

## Counting the way people see it

Text can contain combined characters: an accented letter typed as two parts, a flag made of two symbols, or an emoji with a skin-tone modifier. toolwise counts these as one character each, matching what appears on the screen, which is how most people expect counting to work.
