## What this tool does

The voice recorder turns your browser into a simple audio recorder. Record voice notes, interviews, lectures, podcasts drafts, music ideas or pronunciation practice, then play them back and download them. You can pause and resume, and make several recordings in a row. Everything stays on your device.

## How to record your voice

1. Press **Start recording**. The first time, your browser asks for permission to use the microphone; choose **Allow**.
2. Speak. The moving line shows that sound is being picked up, and the timer shows the length.
3. Use **Pause** and **Resume** if you need a break.
4. Press **Stop and save**. Your recording appears below with a player.
5. Press **Download** to save the file to your device.

## Tips for clear recordings

- **Find a quiet space.** Turn off fans and close windows. Rooms with curtains, carpets and furniture echo less than empty rooms.
- **Keep a steady distance** from the microphone, about 10 to 20 cm.
- **Do a short test first.** Record a few seconds and listen back before a long take.
- **Avoid tapping** the desk or phone, which the microphone picks up easily.
- **Use headphones** if music or another person is playing through your speakers.

## Ideas for using the recorder

- Practicing a speech or presentation and listening back to improve.
- Recording language practice to check your pronunciation.
- Capturing ideas, to-do lists and reminders as voice notes.
- Recording a short message or greeting to send to someone.
