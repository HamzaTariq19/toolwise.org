## When to use it

The voice recorder records audio from your microphone straight in your browser, with no app to install. Use it for voice memos and reminders, practising a speech or interview answers, recording a quick podcast clip or voice-over, capturing a lecture or meeting (with permission), or sending a message to someone who prefers listening to reading.

## How to record your voice

1. Press **Start recording** and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
2. Speak. The waveform moves with your voice and the timer counts up.
3. Press **Pause** to take a break and **Resume** to carry on in the same recording.
4. Press **Stop and save**. The recording appears under **Your recordings** with a player, its length and size.
5. Listen back, then press **Download** to save it. Use the bin button to delete a take you don't want.

Each recording is named with the date and time, such as recording-2026-11-12-174512.webm.

## A worked example

You're preparing a two-minute talk for a job interview. Record a first attempt, play it back, and notice you rushed the opening and said "um" often. Record a second take, pausing between sections to check your notes. The second version runs 1:58. Download it and listen again on your walk to the interview to settle your nerves.

## Tips for clear recordings

- **Get close to the microphone:** about 15 to 30 cm for a laptop or phone.
- **Choose a quiet, soft room.** Curtains, carpets and furniture reduce echo; empty rooms sound hollow.
- **Turn off fans, notifications and music** before you start.
- **Use a headset or plug-in microphone** for noticeably better quality.
- **Do a 5-second test first** and play it back before recording something long.
- **Rename files after downloading,** for example "interview-answer-take-2", so you can find the best take later.

## Common mistakes

- **Forgetting to download.** Recordings aren't saved when you close the tab.
- **Recording others without permission.** In many places, recording a conversation needs everyone's consent.
- **Holding the phone too far away,** which picks up more room noise than voice.
- **Letting the phone lock** during long recordings, which can stop the microphone.

## Private by design

Your audio never leaves your device unless you choose to download and share it.
