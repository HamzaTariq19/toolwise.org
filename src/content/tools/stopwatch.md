## What this tool does

This online stopwatch measures elapsed time with hundredth-of-a-second precision. Record as many laps as you need, see each lap's split and running total, and copy the results to paste into a spreadsheet or message. It works on any phone, tablet or computer, with no app to install.

## How to use it

1. Press **Start** to begin timing.
2. Press **Lap** to record a split while the stopwatch keeps running.
3. Press **Stop** to pause. Press **Resume** to continue from the same time.
4. When stopped, press **Reset** to clear the time and laps.

On a computer, use the **Space** bar to start and stop, **L** for laps and **R** to reset.

## Popular uses

- **Sports and fitness:** time running laps, swimming lengths, interval sets and rest periods.
- **Cooking:** time steps that do not have a fixed duration, like how long dough takes to rise.
- **Presentations and speeches:** practice and see how long each section takes using laps.
- **Classrooms and experiments:** measure reaction times or how long a reaction takes.
- **Work:** track how long tasks take to improve estimates.

## Lap times explained

Each lap row shows two numbers. **Lap time** is how long that single lap took. **Total** is the time on the clock when you pressed Lap. Comparing lap times shows whether you are keeping a steady pace; the fastest lap is highlighted in green and the slowest in red.
