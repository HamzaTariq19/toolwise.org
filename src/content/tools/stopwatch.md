## When to use it

The online stopwatch measures elapsed time to a hundredth of a second, with laps. Use it to time runs, swimming lengths or interval workouts, speeches and presentation rehearsals, science experiments, cooking steps, games, or how long a task really takes you. It works on phones, tablets and computers without installing anything.

## How to use the stopwatch

1. Press **Start**. The display counts up in minutes, seconds and hundredths.
2. Press **Lap** to record a split without stopping. Each lap appears in the table with its **Lap time** and the running **Total**.
3. Press **Stop** (the same button) to pause, and **Start** again to continue.
4. When the stopwatch is stopped, the **Lap** button becomes **Reset**: press it to clear the time and laps.
5. Press **Copy laps** to copy the results as text.

**Keyboard:** **Space** starts or stops, **L** records a lap, and **R** resets.

## A worked example

You're running four laps of a 400 m track. Start the stopwatch at the line and press **Lap** each time you pass it. Your laps come out as 1:52.40, 1:55.10, 1:58.75 and 1:50.20, for a total of 7:36.45. The last lap is highlighted green as your fastest and the third red as your slowest, showing you slowed in the middle and finished strong. Copy the laps into your training log.

## Ideas for using laps

- **Interval training:** record each work and rest period.
- **Presentation practice:** press Lap at each new slide to see which sections run long.
- **Study sessions:** lap at the end of each task to see where time goes.
- **Classroom experiments:** record repeated trials and copy them into a spreadsheet.
- **Board games:** time each player's turn.

## Tips

- **Use the keyboard on a laptop;** Space and L are quicker and more precise than clicking.
- **Put your phone where you can tap it easily** for workouts, and keep the screen on.
- **Copy laps before resetting,** because reset clears them.
- **For counting down instead of up,** use the Countdown Timer.

## Common mistakes

- **Pressing Reset instead of Lap** in a hurry. Reset only works when stopped, which helps avoid this.
- **Starting late.** Get your finger ready on the button or Space bar before the event begins.
- **Relying on it for official records.** Human reaction time adds about 0.2 seconds; races use electronic timing.

## Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
